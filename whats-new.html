Version 1.1

November 16, 2026

Out now

Field Guides, and a map you can sift

Placemoir 1.1 adds Field Guides, places worth a trip in 14 US cities and the Great Smoky Mountains, and a Map tab that stays readable as your journal grows.

Highlights

Field Guides

A new Guides tab with a week of places in each city, picked by friends of Placemoir. Save the ones you like to Want to go. Free for everyone, and they work offline. Browse the guides.

Map filters

Chips on the Map narrow it to a kind of place or a verdict. Want to go places stay hidden until you turn them on, so a big Google Maps import no longer buries the places you’ve been.

Near me

The places on the map as a list, closest first, with how far away each one is. It measures from you when Placemoir already has your location, and from the middle of the map when it doesn’t.

Tidy pins, and Apple Maps

Pins that would overlap gather into one numbered circle; tap it to zoom in. A button beside the place you picked opens its listing in Apple Maps.

Have I been here?

Before you type, Search shows your journal entries within a short walk of where you’re standing. Type and it also finds Field Guides, their places and your Want to go list.

Capture, then finish

Long-press + to Take photo or Record voice memo. The journal entry opens straight away and asks “Use this location?”, so you can write it up while you’re still there.

Also in 1.1

  • Journal entries, by name. What you log about a place is now called a journal entry, everywhere in the app.
  • A steady map on iPad. The Map tab no longer freezes. It opens framed on your places and stays put.
  • Not sure yet. Places you haven’t made up your mind about say so on the map, with Maybe and No in colors of their own.
  • Sort and Filter, apart. The Journal has its own Sort and Filter chips, and the count at the top follows the filters.
  • Times on the place’s clock. A journal entry shows the time where the place is, not where your phone was set.
  • Airports. A new category, for the layovers worth remembering.
  • Backups you can count on. Restoring the same backup on two iPhones keeps every entry, and a damaged or oversized file is turned away with a clear message.
  • Plain answers about iCloud. Sync status says what is really happening, and App Lock covers more screens, with reminders waiting until you unlock.
  • Premium, monthly too. The monthly plan sits next to the annual one, and Restore Purchases unlocks Premium right away.
  • Faster and lighter. Large imports, photos, Search and the Map use less memory, temperatures default to °F in the US, and VoiceOver gets better labels and headings throughout.

Version 1.0

November 6, 2026

The first edition

Placemoir 1.0 is a field journal for places: the restaurants, cafés, hotels, parks and museums you visit. Where you went, and what you really thought, written for you and nobody else.

Highlights

Rate it your way

Simple rating is one tap: I’ll be back, or Never again. Detailed rating is half a star to five, plus “Would you go back?” Switch any time.

Quick capture

Long-press + to save where you are in a couple of seconds. Finish the entry later, and a reminder brings you back to it.

The weather that day

Apple Weather fills in what the sky was doing when you were there, even if you write the entry up a week later.

WITH PLACEMOIR PREMIUM

Photos, voice memos and files

Keep them with the journal entry. Tap a photo to swipe through the rest. Entries with a voice memo show a small waveform, and you can filter to just those.

A map and a memory

Every place on one map: green for go back, plum for skip it, gold for Want to go. Search finds a place by its name, the neighborhood or a word in your notes.

Every journal entry, together

Go back somewhere and it gets a second journal entry with its own date, rating and notes. On this day brings back where you were a year ago.

Bring what you have

Import from Photos and Google Maps for free, or from any other app with an AI assistant’s help through the open archive format.

Yours, and only yours

No account, no ads, no tracking. Your journal syncs through your own iCloud, locks with Face ID, and exports in full whenever you like.

Also in 1.0

  • Made for every screen. iPhone, iPad and iPhone Duo, in light and dark, with Liquid Glass controls.
  • Built for VoiceOver and Voice Control. Icons grow with your text size, buttons are easy to hit, and long addresses never get cut off.
  • Want to go. Save the places friends recommend, then turn them into entries once you’ve been.
  • Ready to type. The keyboard comes up on its own in Search and when you start a new entry.
  • A lock that covers everything. Face ID hides your journal behind open sheets, the share sheet and the app switcher too.
  • A clean slate, everywhere. Delete all data clears your journal from every device on your iCloud account, with a chance to export first.
  • A second app icon. Swap the coastal map for the moss bookmark, with Placemoir Premium.

Free and Premium

Placemoir is free for up to 25 journal entries. Placemoir Premium adds unlimited journal entries, weather and the bookmark icon for $9.99 a year or $1.29 a month (USD), with Family Sharing. Reading, editing, every import, export and Delete all data stay free, so nothing gets locked away if a subscription ends.
