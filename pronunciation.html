Pronunciation

How do you say Placemoir?

The short answer: “place”, then the end of “memoir”.

PLACE-mwahr

IPA, American
/ˈpleɪs.mwɑːr/
IPA, British
/ˈpleɪs.mwɑː/
Syllables
2 · place · moir
Stress
On the first, PLACE
Why is it said that way?

Placemoir is “place” plus “memoir”. Memoir came into English from the French mémoire, memory, and kept its French ending: the “-oir” is said “-wahr”, as in memoir, boudoir and reservoir.

So Placemoir is a memoir of places, and it sounds like one: drop the “me” from memoir and put “place” in front.

What are the common mix-ups?
  • place-MORE: the “-oir” is “-wahr”, not “-or”.
  • place-MOY-er: “oi” here is the French “wah”, not “oy” as in boy.
  • pla-SEM-wahr: it starts with “place”, one syllable.
  • place-MWAHR: close, but the stress goes on PLACE, like MEM-wahr.

Any of these will still find you the right app, and I won’t mind.

How do you write it?

Placemoir: one word, with a capital P and the rest lowercase. Not PlaceMoir, Place Moir or PLACEMOIR. The logo is an all-lowercase italic wordmark, but in a sentence the name takes a capital P like any other.

Siri said it differently.

Siri and VoiceOver read app names with their own voices, and a new word can come out a little off. The way it’s written on this page is how I say it.
