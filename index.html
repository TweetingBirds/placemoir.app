Questions

Good to know

Is Placemoir a review app?

Not in the usual sense. Your ratings and notes are for you alone. Nothing you write is published, and there's no way for other people to see your journal. Think of it as a notebook, not a review site.

What kinds of places can I keep?

Anywhere you'd want to remember: restaurants, cafés and bars, hotels and other stays, parks, museums, attractions, shops, or a bench with a good view. Each entry holds the place, the date and time, the weather, your rating and notes, and any photos, voice memos or files you add.

Do I need an account?

No. Placemoir has no accounts. Your journal is stored on your device and syncs through your own iCloud account to your other iPhone and iPad.

Can anyone else see my journal?

No. Your entries stay on your devices and in your personal iCloud account, which we can't access. You can also lock the app with Face ID.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone, iPad and iPhone Duo. On iPad and an open iPhone Duo, your journal and the entry you're reading sit side by side.

Can I get my journal out?

Yes. Export your whole journal at any time from the You tab. You get every entry in a standard format, along with all of your photos, voice memos and files.

Where can I get it, and what does it cost?

Placemoir is on the App Store for iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 26 or later. It’s free to download and free for your first 25 visit entries (one for each time you log a place). Placemoir Premium, $9.99 a year or $1.29 a month in the US with Family Sharing, unlocks unlimited visit entries, the weather that day and the bookmark app icon. Importing, exporting and deleting your data are always free.
