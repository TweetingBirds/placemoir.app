What Placemoir does

Placemoir is a private journal for the places you visit. It lets you:

  • Save places, such as restaurants, hotels, parks, museums and attractions, using Apple Maps search or your current location
  • Import places from the photos you pick or from a Google Maps export
  • Log visits with the date, time, weather, your rating and notes
  • Attach photos, voice memos and files to a visit
  • See your places on a map, search your journal, and keep a Want to go list
  • Lock the App with Face ID
  • Export your journal, or delete all of it
  • Optionally sync your journal across your devices with iCloud

Some features depend on your device, operating system, region or settings, and may not be available everywhere.
