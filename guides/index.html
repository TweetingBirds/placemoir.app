Field Guides

Places worth the trip.

Places to stay, eat, see and walk, in a city or out on the trail, picked by people who've been. Add a Field Guide to your Want to go list in Placemoir, then rate each place once you've gone.

Cities

42 places

Atlanta from Mary Mac's to the BeltLine

Seven days of civil rights history, Southern cooking, BeltLine walks and a few places to stay, from Sweet Auburn to the Westside.

38 places

Live music and swimming holes in Austin

Seven days of brisket and breakfast tacos, live music every night, spring-fed swimming holes and bats over Lady Bird Lake.

41 places

Boston on foot

Seven days of the Freedom Trail and the North End, oysters and cannoli, great museums, Fenway and walks along the Charles.

43 places

Chicago by the lake

Seven days of architecture by boat, deep dish and Italian beef, the Art Institute and the lakefront, blues bars and a game at Wrigley.

36 places

A mile high in Denver

Seven days of mountain views and red rocks, green chile and craft beer, great museums and murals, with a day up in the foothills.

38 places

Barbecue and jazz in Kansas City

Seven days of barbecue and jazz, fountains and boulevards, the WWI museum and the Nelson-Atkins, with a Chiefs or Royals game if you can.

38 places

Nashville beyond Broadway

Seven days of hot chicken and meat-and-threes, honky-tonks and the Opry, songwriter nights, and green trails just outside town.

46 places

New York by ferry and on foot

Seven days of museums and delis, bridges and harbor ferries, Central Park and the High Line, across Manhattan and Brooklyn.

37 places

Theme parks and springs in Orlando

Seven days of theme parks and the space coast, springs and manatees, and the quieter, tree-lined side of Orlando and Winter Park.

36 places

Portland rain or shine

Seven days of food carts and doughnuts, roses and a Japanese garden, books and coffee, and waterfalls in the Columbia River Gorge.

39 places

Salt Lake City under the Wasatch

Seven days of mountain trails, pioneer history, mole and fry sauce, and a day out on the Great Salt Lake, with the Wasatch always in view.

44 places

San Francisco in the fog

Seven days of cable cars and fog, dim sum and burritos, bay crossings and big parks, from the Ferry Building to Lands End.

42 places

Seattle by the Sound

Seven days of Pike Place, ferries and salmon, glass art and rock history, and green parks with views of the Sound and the mountains.

40 places

Free museums and half-smokes in DC

Seven days of free museums and monuments, half-smokes and Ethiopian dinners, Georgetown walks and cherry trees on the Tidal Basin.

National parks

29 places

Great Smoky Mountains from Cades Cove to Kuwohi

Three or four days in the country's most visited national park: a hike-in lodge, waterfall walks, old farm valleys full of deer and elk, and pancakes in the towns at either end.
