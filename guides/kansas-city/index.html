Barbecue and jazz in Kansas City

Seven days of barbecue and jazz, fountains and boulevards, the WWI museum and the Nelson-Atkins, with a Chiefs or Royals game if you can.

Hotel · Downtown

Hotel Kansas City

A 1923 Tudor-style gentlemen's club turned hotel, with a dark, cozy bar downstairs.

1228 Baltimore Ave, Kansas City, MO 64105

Hotel · Country Club Plaza

The Raphael Hotel

A small 1927 hotel across from the Plaza, with fountains and shopping out the door.

325 Ward Pkwy, Kansas City, MO 64112

Hotel · Country Club Plaza

The Fontaine

A modern hotel on the Plaza with a rooftop pool and bar looking over the fountains.

901 W 48th St, Kansas City, MO 64112

Hotel · Crossroads

Crossroads Hotel

A converted 1911 brick warehouse in the arts district, with a rooftop bar and a restaurant downstairs.

2101 Central St, Kansas City, MO 64108

Restaurant · Kansas City, Kansas

Joe's Kansas City Bar-B-Que

Barbecue in a working gas station. Order the Z-Man sandwich, and expect a line out the door.

3002 W 47th Ave, Kansas City, KS 66103

Restaurant · 18th and Vine

Arthur Bryant's Barbeque

The burnt ends and sauce that made Kansas City barbecue famous. Close to the jazz district.

1727 Brooklyn Ave, Kansas City, MO 64127

Restaurant · Midtown

Gates Bar-B-Q

Hear the counter staff shout "Hi, may I help you?" as you walk in. Get the ribs.

3205 Main St, Kansas City, MO 64111

Restaurant · Westport

Q39

Competition-style barbecue with a sit-down room. The burnt end burger is a favorite.

1000 W 39th St, Kansas City, MO 64111

Restaurant · Crossroads

Jack Stack Barbecue

The Freight House location, with a long bar and crown prime beef ribs. Book for dinner.

101 W 22nd St, Kansas City, MO 64108

Restaurant · East Kansas City

LC's Bar-B-Q

A smoky little room with a pit you can see from the door. Burnt ends on white bread.

5800 Dr Martin Luther King Jr Blvd, Kansas City, MO 64129

Restaurant · Crossroads

Corvino Supper Club

Japanese-influenced plates with live jazz in the supper club, or the tasting room for a splurge.

1830 Walnut St, Kansas City, MO 64108

Restaurant · Crossroads

The Rieger

Pork soup and seasonal cooking in a 1915 hotel bar. The Manhattan Club is downstairs.

1924 Main St, Kansas City, MO 64108

Restaurant · Crossroads

Town Topic Hamburgers

A tiny 24-hour burger counter since 1937. Sliders, fries and a malt.

2021 Broadway Blvd, Kansas City, MO 64108

Restaurant · River Market

City Market

A farmers market since 1857, with year-round shops and restaurants. Best on a Saturday morning.

20 E 5th St, Kansas City, MO 64106

Café · Crossroads

Messenger Coffee

A three-story roastery and bakery with a rooftop. A good start to a Crossroads day.

1624 Grand Blvd, Kansas City, MO 64108

Café · Crossroads

Christopher Elbow Chocolates

Jewel-like chocolates and an excellent drinking chocolate.

1819 McGee St, Kansas City, MO 64108

Café · Waldo

Betty Rae's Ice Cream

Small-batch ice cream with a line on summer nights. Try a scoop of whatever's new.

7140 Wornall Rd, Kansas City, MO 64114

Bar · Crossroads

Tom's Town Distilling Co.

An Art Deco distillery named for the city's Prohibition boss. Gin cocktails and tours.

1701 Main St, Kansas City, MO 64108

Bar · Crossroads

Green Lady Lounge

Live jazz every night, no cover, in a dark red room.

1809 Grand Blvd, Kansas City, MO 64108

Bar · Westside

Boulevard Brewing Co.

Kansas City's brewery. Take the tour and finish at the rooftop tasting room.

2501 Southwest Blvd, Kansas City, MO 64108

Museum · Crown Center

National WWI Museum and Memorial

The country's museum of the Great War, and outstanding. Ride up the tower for the skyline view.

2 Memorial Dr, Kansas City, MO 64108

Museum · Southmoreland

Nelson-Atkins Museum of Art

A free, world-class museum. Find the giant shuttlecocks on the lawn.

4525 Oak St, Kansas City, MO 64111

Museum · Southmoreland

Kemper Museum of Contemporary Art

A free contemporary art museum next to the Nelson-Atkins, with a giant spider out front.

4420 Warwick Blvd, Kansas City, MO 64111

Museum · 18th and Vine

American Jazz Museum

Charlie Parker, Count Basie and the Kansas City sound. The Blue Room jazz club is next door.

1616 E 18th St, Kansas City, MO 64108

Museum · 18th and Vine

Negro Leagues Baseball Museum

The story of Black baseball, ending on a field of bronze players. One of the best small museums anywhere.

1616 E 18th St, Kansas City, MO 64108

Attraction · 18th and Vine

Mutual Musicians Foundation

A musicians' union hall with a late-night jam session on weekends, going until dawn.

1823 Highland Ave, Kansas City, MO 64108

Attraction · Crown Center

Union Station Kansas City

A grand 1914 station with a 95-foot ceiling, science exhibits and the free streetcar outside.

30 W Pershing Rd, Kansas City, MO 64108

Museum · River Market

Arabia Steamboat Museum

Cargo from an 1856 steamboat dug out of a Kansas cornfield, perfectly preserved.

400 Grand Blvd, Kansas City, MO 64106

Attraction · Country Club Plaza

Country Club Plaza

A 1920s shopping district modeled on Seville, full of fountains. The holiday lights start on Thanksgiving.

4706 Broadway Blvd, Kansas City, MO 64112

Attraction · Crossroads

Kauffman Center for the Performing Arts

Two shell-shaped halls for the symphony, opera and ballet. Free tours on some days.

1601 Broadway Blvd, Kansas City, MO 64108

Museum · Independence

Harry S. Truman Library and Museum

Truman's presidency, from the atomic bomb to the Marshall Plan. His home is nearby.

500 W US-24, Independence, MO 64050

Attraction · Truman Sports Complex

GEHA Field at Arrowhead Stadium

Home of the Chiefs and the loudest crowd in football. Come early for the tailgates.

1 Arrowhead Dr, Kansas City, MO 64129

Attraction · Truman Sports Complex

Kauffman Stadium

The Royals' ballpark, with a fountain wall in the outfield.

1 Royal Way, Kansas City, MO 64129

Loose Park

A big rose garden and a pond near the Plaza. Bring a picnic.

5200 Wornall Rd, Kansas City, MO 64112

Park · Crown Center

Penn Valley Park

Rolling lawns below the WWI memorial, with the best view of the downtown skyline.

Penn Valley Park, Kansas City, MO 64108

Park · Swope Park

Swope Park

One of the country's biggest city parks, with the zoo, trails and the Starlight Theatre.

Swope Park, Kansas City, MO 64132

Park · Overland Park

Overland Park Arboretum and Botanical Gardens

Woodland trails and gardens on the Kansas side. The Monet garden is lovely in summer.

8909 W 179th St, Overland Park, KS 66013

Shawnee Mission Park

A big lake with paddleboards and kayaks, and a tall observation tower.

7900 Renner Rd, Shawnee, KS 66219
