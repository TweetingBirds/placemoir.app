Free museums and half-smokes in DC

Seven days of free museums and monuments, half-smokes and Ethiopian dinners, Georgetown walks and cherry trees on the Tidal Basin.

Hotel · Lafayette Square

The Hay-Adams

Across Lafayette Square from the White House. Ask for a room facing it, then have a drink in Off the Record downstairs.

800 16th St NW, Washington, DC 20006

Hotel · Dupont Circle

The Jefferson

A quiet, elegant 1923 hotel, with Jefferson's letters on the walls and a cozy bar.

1200 16th St NW, Washington, DC 20036

Hotel · Penn Quarter

Kimpton Hotel Monaco Washington DC

A marble 1839 post office in Penn Quarter, steps from the Portrait Gallery and the Metro.

700 F St NW, Washington, DC 20004

Hotel · Foggy Bottom

Hotel Hive

Tiny, well-designed rooms near the Lincoln Memorial. The budget pick, with a rooftop bar.

2224 F St NW, Washington, DC 20037

Restaurant · U Street

Ben's Chili Bowl

The chili half-smoke, served on U Street since 1958.

1213 U St NW, Washington, DC 20009

Restaurant · Downtown

Old Ebbitt Grill

The city's oldest saloon, a block from the White House. Go for oysters at the late-night happy hour.

675 15th St NW, Washington, DC 20005

Restaurant · Penn Quarter

Rasika

Modern Indian cooking. The crispy spinach, palak chaat, is famous for a reason.

633 D St NW, Washington, DC 20004

Restaurant · Penn Quarter

Zaytinya

José Andrés's Greek, Turkish and Lebanese small plates in a bright, airy room.

701 9th St NW, Washington, DC 20001

Restaurant · Logan Circle

Le Diplomate

A Paris brasserie on 14th Street. Book weekend brunch ahead.

1601 14th St NW, Washington, DC 20009

Restaurant · U Street

Compass Rose

Street food from around the world in a cozy rowhouse. Order the khachapuri.

1346 T St NW, Washington, DC 20009

Restaurant · Foggy Bottom

Founding Farmers

Big, comforting American plates from a restaurant owned by farmers. Good for breakfast.

1924 Pennsylvania Ave NW, Washington, DC 20006

Restaurant · Georgetown

Martin's Tavern

A Georgetown tavern since 1933, where JFK is said to have proposed to Jackie in booth three.

1264 Wisconsin Ave NW, Washington, DC 20007

Restaurant · NoMa

Union Market

A food hall in an old wholesale market. Graze a little from everywhere.

1309 5th St NE, Washington, DC 20002

Restaurant · Capitol Hill

Eastern Market

A market hall since 1873. Get the blueberry buckwheat pancakes at the Market Lunch counter on a weekend.

225 7th St SE, Washington, DC 20003

Café · Georgetown

Call Your Mother

A Jew-ish deli with bagels people line up for. The Georgetown shop is a tiny pink house.

3428 O St NW, Washington, DC 20007

Café · Georgetown

Baked & Wired

Cupcakes and coffee by the C&O Canal, a calmer pick than the famous cupcake line nearby.

1052 Thomas Jefferson St NW, Washington, DC 20007

Café · Dupont Circle

Kramers

An independent bookstore with a cafe and bar at the back, open late.

1517 Connecticut Ave NW, Washington, DC 20036

Bar · Downtown

Round Robin Bar

The Willard hotel's round bar, where Henry Clay mixed his first mint julep in Washington.

1401 Pennsylvania Ave NW, Washington, DC 20004

See

Attraction · National Mall

Lincoln Memorial

Go at night, when it's lit and quiet, and read the Gettysburg Address on the wall.

2 Lincoln Memorial Cir NW, Washington, DC 20002

Attraction · National Mall

Washington Monument

Free timed tickets to the top. Reserve online a day or two ahead.

2 15th St NW, Washington, DC 20024

Attraction · Capitol Hill

United States Capitol

Free tours from the visitor center. Book ahead, and add the galleries through your representative.

First St SE, Washington, DC 20004

Attraction · Capitol Hill

Library of Congress

The Jefferson Building's Great Hall is breathtaking. Take the tunnel from the Capitol.

101 Independence Ave SE, Washington, DC 20540

Attraction · Downtown

The White House

See it from Lafayette Square or the Ellipse. Tours are requested through your member of Congress.

1600 Pennsylvania Ave NW, Washington, DC 20500

Museum · National Mall

National Museum of African American History and Culture

Start at the bottom, in the history galleries, and work your way up. Allow most of a day.

1400 Constitution Ave NW, Washington, DC 20560

Museum · National Mall

National Air and Space Museum

The Wright Flyer, Apollo 11 and the Spirit of St. Louis. Free, with timed passes.

650 Jefferson Dr SW, Washington, DC 20560

Museum · National Mall

National Museum of Natural History

The Hope Diamond, the dinosaur hall and the giant squid. Free.

10th St NW & Constitution Ave NW, Washington, DC 20560

Museum · National Mall

National Gallery of Art

The only Leonardo in the Americas, and a sculpture garden with an ice rink in winter.

Constitution Ave NW, Washington, DC 20565

Museum · Penn Quarter

National Portrait Gallery

The presidents, including the Obama portraits, and a glass-roofed courtyard to rest in.

8th St NW & F St NW, Washington, DC 20001

Museum · National Mall

United States Holocaust Memorial Museum

Powerful and hard. Free timed passes are needed from March to August.

100 Raoul Wallenberg Pl SW, Washington, DC 20024

Museum · Penn Quarter

Ford's Theatre

Where Lincoln was shot in 1865, still a working theater, with the Petersen House across the street.

511 10th St NW, Washington, DC 20004

Attraction · Cathedral Heights

Washington National Cathedral

Gothic arches, a Darth Vader gargoyle and views from the tower. Look for the moon rock in the space window.

3101 Wisconsin Ave NW, Washington, DC 20016

Attraction · Arlington

Arlington National Cemetery

The Kennedy graves and the changing of the guard at the Tomb of the Unknown Soldier.

Arlington, VA 22211

Outside

Park · National Mall

National Mall

Two miles from the Capitol to the Lincoln Memorial. Walk it end to end once, then use the Circulator.

National Mall, Washington, DC 20024

Park · National Mall

Tidal Basin

The cherry trees in early spring, and the Jefferson and MLK memorials all year. Rent a paddle boat.

Tidal Basin, Washington, DC 20024

Park · Tidal Basin

Martin Luther King Jr. Memorial

Dr. King carved from a Stone of Hope, with his words along the wall behind.

1850 W Basin Dr SW, Washington, DC 20024

Park · Capitol Hill

United States Botanic Garden

A free glasshouse jungle at the foot of the Capitol. Good on a rainy or cold day.

100 Maryland Ave SW, Washington, DC 20001

Park · Georgetown

Georgetown Waterfront Park

Fountains and river views, with kayaks for rent at Key Bridge Boathouse.

3303 Water St NW, Washington, DC 20007

Park · Georgetown

Dumbarton Oaks

Terraced gardens behind a Georgetown mansion. Best in spring.

1703 32nd St NW, Washington, DC 20007

Park · Rock Creek

Rock Creek Park

Woods and trails right in the city. On weekends Beach Drive is closed to cars.

Rock Creek Park, Washington, DC 20008

Park · Potomac River

Theodore Roosevelt Island

A wooded island in the Potomac with an easy loop trail and a statue of Teddy Roosevelt.

Arlington, VA 22209
