A week in San Francisco

Seven days of cable cars and fog, dim sum and burritos, bay crossings and big parks, from the Ferry Building to Lands End.

44 places · Picked by friends of Placemoir · Checked November 8, 2026

Stay

Hotel · Nob Hill

Fairmont San Francisco

The grand hotel on top of Nob Hill, with cable cars at the door and a tiki bar in the basement.

950 Mason St, San Francisco, CA 94108

Hotel · SoMa

Palace Hotel

Have a drink under the glass dome of the Garden Court, even if you stay somewhere else.

2 New Montgomery St, San Francisco, CA 94105

Hotel · Pacific Heights

Hotel Drisco

A quiet 1903 hotel in Pacific Heights, with breakfast and views over the bay.

2901 Pacific Ave, San Francisco, CA 94115

Hotel · Fisherman's Wharf

Hotel Zephyr

A playful nautical hotel at the wharf, handy for the Alcatraz ferry and the cable cars.

250 Beach St, San Francisco, CA 94133

Restaurant · Nob Hill

Swan Oyster Depot

A marble counter, a few stools and the best crab and oysters in town. Lunch only, cash only, always a line.

1517 Polk St, San Francisco, CA 94109

Restaurant · Financial District

Tadich Grill

Open since 1849. Sit at the counter and order the cioppino.

240 California St, San Francisco, CA 94111

Restaurant · Embarcadero

Hog Island Oyster Co.

Tomales Bay oysters with a view of the Bay Bridge. Get there before noon.

1 Ferry Building, San Francisco, CA 94111

Restaurant · Mission

La Taqueria

The Mission burrito, no rice. Order it dorado style, crisped on the grill.

2889 Mission St, San Francisco, CA 94110

Café · Mission

Tartine Bakery

Morning buns and country bread worth the queue. Then carry them to Dolores Park.

600 Guerrero St, San Francisco, CA 94110

Restaurant · Hayes Valley

Zuni Café

The roast chicken with bread salad takes an hour, so order it when you sit down.

1658 Market St, San Francisco, CA 94102

Restaurant · Nob Hill

House of Prime Rib

Prime rib carved tableside from silver carts since 1949. Book ahead.

1906 Van Ness Ave, San Francisco, CA 94109

Restaurant · Western Addition

State Bird Provisions

Small plates rolled past on carts, dim sum style. Fun, and different every night.

1529 Fillmore St, San Francisco, CA 94115

Restaurant · NoPa

Nopa

A big, lively room serving wood-fired California food late into the night.

560 Divisadero St, San Francisco, CA 94117

Restaurant · Chinatown

Good Mong Kok Bakery

Takeout dim sum by the box. Har gow and pork buns for a few dollars.

1039 Stockton St, San Francisco, CA 94108

Restaurant · North Beach

Mama's on Washington Square

A North Beach breakfast institution. The line moves, and the French toast is worth it.

1701 Stockton St, San Francisco, CA 94133

Café · North Beach

Liguria Bakery

Focaccia only, since 1911, until it sells out. Go before 11.

1700 Stockton St, San Francisco, CA 94133

Restaurant · Inner Richmond

Burma Superstar

Burmese food on Clement Street. The tea leaf salad is a must.

309 Clement St, San Francisco, CA 94118

Café · Bernal Heights

Mitchell's Ice Cream

Tropical flavors like ube and mango since 1953. Take a number.

688 San Jose Ave, San Francisco, CA 94110

Café · SoMa

Sightglass Coffee

A big, airy roastery in SoMa. Watch the roasting from the mezzanine.

270 7th St, San Francisco, CA 94103

Bar · North Beach

Vesuvio Cafe

The Beat poets' bar, across Jack Kerouac Alley from City Lights Books.

255 Columbus Ave, San Francisco, CA 94133

Bar · Fisherman's Wharf

The Buena Vista

Where Irish coffee came to America. Watch the bartenders line up a dozen at a time.

2765 Hyde St, San Francisco, CA 94109

Bar · Mission

Trick Dog

Inventive cocktails in the Mission, with a menu that changes theme twice a year.

3010 20th St, San Francisco, CA 94110

Attraction · San Francisco Bay

Alcatraz Island

The audio tour of the old prison is excellent. Book the ferry from Pier 33 weeks ahead.

Alcatraz Island, San Francisco, CA 94133

Attraction · Presidio

Golden Gate Bridge

Walk or bike across, at least to the first tower. Bring a jacket, whatever the season.

Golden Gate Bridge, San Francisco, CA 94129

Attraction · Embarcadero

Ferry Building Marketplace

Local food under one roof. Come on a Saturday morning for the farmers market.

1 Ferry Building, San Francisco, CA 94111

Museum · Nob Hill

Cable Car Museum

A free museum inside the working powerhouse that pulls every cable car in the city.

1201 Mason St, San Francisco, CA 94108

Attraction · Telegraph Hill

Coit Tower

1930s murals and a 360-degree view. Walk down the Filbert Steps afterwards.

1 Telegraph Hill Blvd, San Francisco, CA 94133

Museum · SoMa

San Francisco Museum of Modern Art

Seven floors of modern art. The ground floor galleries are free.

151 3rd St, San Francisco, CA 94103

Museum · Civic Center

Asian Art Museum

One of the largest collections of Asian art in the West, in the old main library.

200 Larkin St, San Francisco, CA 94102

Museum · Golden Gate Park

de Young Museum

American art and textiles in Golden Gate Park. The tower's observation floor is free.

50 Hagiwara Tea Garden Dr, San Francisco, CA 94118

Museum · Golden Gate Park

California Academy of Sciences

A rainforest, an aquarium and a planetarium under a living roof.

55 Music Concourse Dr, San Francisco, CA 94118

Museum · Lincoln Park

Legion of Honor

European art and Rodin's Thinker, on a cliff above the Golden Gate.

100 34th Ave, San Francisco, CA 94121

Museum · Embarcadero

Exploratorium

Hundreds of hands-on science exhibits on a pier. Thursday nights are adults only.

Pier 15, The Embarcadero, San Francisco, CA 94111

Attraction · Fisherman's Wharf

Musée Mécanique

A free hall of antique arcade machines. Bring quarters.

Pier 45, San Francisco, CA 94133

Attraction · Mission

Mission Dolores

The oldest building in the city, finished in 1791, with a small cemetery and garden.

3321 16th St, San Francisco, CA 94114

Attraction · Chinatown

Golden Gate Fortune Cookie Factory

Watch fortune cookies folded by hand in a narrow Chinatown alley. Bring cash.

56 Ross Alley, San Francisco, CA 94108

Outside

Park · Golden Gate Park

Golden Gate Park

Three miles of gardens, lakes and bison. Rent a bike and ride it all the way to the ocean.

Golden Gate Park, San Francisco, CA 94122

Park · Golden Gate Park

Japanese Tea Garden

The oldest public Japanese garden in the country. Go early, and have tea in the teahouse.

75 Hagiwara Tea Garden Dr, San Francisco, CA 94118

Park · Outer Richmond

Lands End Lookout

A coastal trail with views of the Golden Gate, ending at the ruins of the Sutro Baths.

680 Point Lobos Ave, San Francisco, CA 94121

Park · Presidio

Crissy Field

A flat bayside walk from the Marina to the foot of the Golden Gate Bridge.

Crissy Field, San Francisco, CA 94129

Park · Mission

Mission Dolores Park

The city's favorite lawn on a sunny afternoon, with a skyline view from the top.

Dolores St, San Francisco, CA 94114

Park · Alamo Square

Alamo Square

The Painted Ladies with downtown behind them. Bring a picnic.

Hayes St, San Francisco, CA 94117

Park · Twin Peaks

Twin Peaks

The whole city at your feet. Go on a clear morning before the fog rolls in.

501 Twin Peaks Blvd, San Francisco, CA 94114

Park · Marin County

Muir Woods National Monument

Old-growth redwoods half an hour north. Parking or shuttle must be booked ahead.

1 Muir Woods Rd, Mill Valley, CA 94941
