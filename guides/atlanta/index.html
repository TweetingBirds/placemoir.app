A week in Atlanta

Seven days of civil rights history, Southern cooking, BeltLine walks and a few places to stay, from Sweet Auburn to the Westside.

43 places · Picked by Nate Bird · Checked November 8, 2026

Stay

Hotel · Midtown

The Georgian Terrace Hotel

A 1911 hotel across the street from the Fox Theatre. Ask for a room facing Peachtree.

659 Peachtree St NE, Atlanta, GA 30308

Hotel · Poncey-Highland

Hotel Clermont

A restored 1924 hotel with a rooftop bar, a short walk from Ponce City Market and the BeltLine.

789 Ponce de Leon Ave NE, Atlanta, GA 30306

Hotel · Downtown

The Candler Hotel Atlanta

Asa Candler's 1906 office tower, now a hotel. Handy for the Centennial Park museums.

127 Peachtree St NE, Atlanta, GA 30303

Hotel · Buckhead

The St. Regis Atlanta

The splurge. Quiet Buckhead, a big pool, and the Atlanta History Center down the road.

88 W Paces Ferry Rd NW, Atlanta, GA 30305

Eat

Restaurant · Midtown

Mary Mac's Tea Room

Southern plates since 1945. Start with the pot likker and cornbread.

224 Ponce de Leon Ave NE, Atlanta, GA 30308

Restaurant · Midtown

The Varsity

Chili dogs, onion rings and a frosted orange, served fast since 1928.

61 North Ave NW, Atlanta, GA 30308

Restaurant · Vine City

Busy Bee Cafe

Fried chicken and sides since 1947. Expect a line at lunch, and it's worth it.

810 Martin Luther King Jr Dr SW, Atlanta, GA 30314

Restaurant · Castleberry Hill

Paschal's

Where civil rights leaders met to plan in the 1960s. Fried chicken and peach cobbler.

180 Northside Dr SW, Atlanta, GA 30313

Restaurant · Candler Park

Fox Bros. Bar-B-Q

Texas-style brisket and smoked wings. Get the Brunswick stew on the side.

1238 DeKalb Ave NE, Atlanta, GA 30307

Restaurant · Home Park

Antico Pizza Napoletana

Wood-fired Neapolitan pizza at shared tables in the middle of the kitchen.

1093 Hemphill Ave NW, Atlanta, GA 30318

Restaurant · Inman Park

Krog Street Market

A food hall in an old warehouse right on the BeltLine. Good when the group can't agree.

99 Krog St NE, Atlanta, GA 30307

Bar · Inman Park

Ticonderoga Club

A small cocktail bar tucked in the back of Krog Street Market, with a short, good food menu.

99 Krog St NE, Atlanta, GA 30307

Restaurant · Glenwood Park

Gunshow

Kevin Gillespie's chefs bring dishes to your table and you say yes or no. Book ahead.

924 Garrett St SE, Atlanta, GA 30316

Restaurant · Westside

Bacchanalia

The special-occasion dinner of the week: a long tasting menu from Anne Quatrano.

1460 Ellsworth Industrial Blvd NW, Atlanta, GA 30318

Restaurant · Westside

Miller Union

Seasonal Southern cooking from Steven Satterfield. The farm egg in celery cream is the one to order.

999 Brady Ave NW, Atlanta, GA 30318

Restaurant · Summerhill

Talat Market

Thai cooking made with Georgia produce. Loud, fun, and spicy if you ask.

112 Ormond St SE, Atlanta, GA 30315

Restaurant · Reynoldstown

Home grown

Breakfast with a side of thrift-store decor. Order the Comfy Chicken Biscuit.

968 Memorial Dr SE, Atlanta, GA 30316

Restaurant · Sweet Auburn

Sweet Auburn Curb Market

A city market since 1918. Lunch from the stalls before the King historic site, five minutes away.

209 Edgewood Ave SE, Atlanta, GA 30303

Café · Buford Highway

Lee's Bakery

Banh mi on fresh baguettes, cheap and quick. A good first stop on Buford Highway.

4005 Buford Hwy NE, Atlanta, GA 30345

Restaurant · Druid Hills

Desta Ethiopian Kitchen

Big platters of Ethiopian stews on injera, with a patio that's busy every night.

3086 Briarcliff Rd NE, Atlanta, GA 30329

Bar · Decatur

Kimball House

Oysters and cocktails in Decatur's old train depot. Go for happy hour.

303 E Howard Ave, Decatur, GA 30030

Restaurant · Buckhead

Bones

An old-school steakhouse open since 1979, with white tablecloths and a deep wine list.

3130 Piedmont Rd NE, Atlanta, GA 30305

See

Attraction · Sweet Auburn

Martin Luther King, Jr. National Historical Park

Dr. King's birth home, Ebenezer Baptist Church and his tomb, all on one street. Book the free birth home tour early in the day.

450 Auburn Ave NE, Atlanta, GA 30312

Museum · Downtown

National Center for Civil and Human Rights

The lunch counter simulation is hard to sit through, and that's the point. Allow two hours.

100 Ivan Allen Jr Blvd NW, Atlanta, GA 30313

Attraction · Downtown

Georgia Aquarium

Whale sharks in one enormous tank. Go at opening to beat the school groups.

225 Baker St NW, Atlanta, GA 30313

Museum · Downtown

World of Coca-Cola

Corporate, but fun. Finish in the tasting room with drinks from around the world.

121 Baker St NW, Atlanta, GA 30313

Museum · Midtown

High Museum of Art

Richard Meier's white building holds a strong folk and self-taught art collection.

1280 Peachtree St NE, Atlanta, GA 30309

Attraction · Midtown

Fox Theatre

A 1929 movie palace with a starry ceiling. See a show, or take the daytime tour.

660 Peachtree St NE, Atlanta, GA 30308

Museum · Buckhead

Atlanta History Center

The Cyclorama, Swan House and 33 acres of gardens. Easily half a day.

130 W Paces Ferry Rd NW, Atlanta, GA 30305

Museum · Poncey-Highland

Jimmy Carter Presidential Library and Museum

A presidency and a long life after it. The Japanese garden out back is a calm stop.

441 John Lewis Freedom Pkwy NE, Atlanta, GA 30307

Attraction · Old Fourth Ward

Ponce City Market

A huge old Sears building with a food hall, shops and a rooftop with skyline views and mini golf.

675 Ponce de Leon Ave NE, Atlanta, GA 30308

Attraction · Grant Park

Historic Oakland Cemetery

Victorian gardens and Margaret Mitchell's grave, with the downtown skyline behind. Lovely at golden hour.

248 Oakland Ave SE, Atlanta, GA 30312

Museum · Midtown

Center for Puppetry Arts

Jim Henson's own Muppets, and puppets from around the world. Better for adults than it sounds.

1404 Spring St NW, Atlanta, GA 30309

Museum · Druid Hills

Fernbank Museum

Big dinosaurs inside, and a walk through an old-growth forest outside.

767 Clifton Rd, Atlanta, GA 30307

Museum · Hapeville

Delta Flight Museum

Walk through a 747 in the old hangars next to the airport. Easy before an evening flight.

1060 Delta Blvd, Atlanta, GA 30354

Attraction · Grant Park

Zoo Atlanta

Gorillas and a leafy setting inside Grant Park. Pair it with Oakland Cemetery.

800 Cherokee Ave SE, Atlanta, GA 30315

Attraction · Doraville

Buford Highway Farmers Market

An international grocery the size of a warehouse. Come hungry and wander the aisles.

5600 Buford Hwy NE, Doraville, GA 30340

Outside

Park · Midtown

Piedmont Park

The city's big lawn, with skyline views over Lake Clara Meer. Pick up a picnic on the way.

400 Park Dr NE, Atlanta, GA 30306

Park · Midtown

Atlanta Botanical Garden

The canopy walk through the treetops, and the orchid house. Check for evening events.

1345 Piedmont Ave NE, Atlanta, GA 30309

Park · Old Fourth Ward

Atlanta BeltLine Eastside Trail

Walk or rent a bike from Piedmont Park to Krog Street, past murals, patios and Ponce City Market.

Atlanta, GA 30308

Park · Old Fourth Ward

Historic Fourth Ward Park

A small park built around a stormwater lake, right off the BeltLine.

680 Dallas St NE, Atlanta, GA 30308

Park · Grove Park

Westside Park

The city's largest park, around an old quarry turned reservoir. Big views, few crowds.

1660 Johnson Rd NW, Atlanta, GA 30318

Park · Chattahoochee River

Cochran Shoals

A flat loop along the Chattahoochee River, part of the national recreation area. Good for a morning run.

Interstate N Pkwy SE, Atlanta, GA 30339
