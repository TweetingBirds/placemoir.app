Boston on foot

Seven days of the Freedom Trail and the North End, oysters and cannoli, great museums, Fenway and walks along the Charles.

Hotel · Downtown

Omni Parker House

America's oldest continuously operating hotel, on the Freedom Trail. Boston cream pie was invented here.

60 School St, Boston, MA 02108

Hotel · Beacon Hill

The Liberty, a Luxury Collection Hotel

A former jail, now a striking hotel at the foot of Beacon Hill. Have a drink in Alibi, in the old drunk tank.

215 Charles St, Boston, MA 02114

Hotel · Back Bay

Fairmont Copley Plaza

A 1912 grand hotel on Copley Square, with a resident Labrador who greets guests.

138 St James Ave, Boston, MA 02116

Hotel · Fenway

The Verb Hotel

A rock-and-roll motel by Fenway Park, with a heated pool and vinyl in every room.

1271 Boylston St, Boston, MA 02215

Restaurant · North End

Neptune Oyster

A tiny oyster bar with the city's best hot lobster roll. Go at opening to skip the wait.

63 Salem St, Boston, MA 02113

Restaurant · Faneuil Hall

Union Oyster House

America's oldest restaurant, serving since 1826. Sit at the raw bar downstairs.

41 Union St, Boston, MA 02108

Restaurant · North End

Giacomo's

Red-sauce Italian in a small, loud room. Cash only, and a line, but it moves.

355 Hanover St, Boston, MA 02113

Restaurant · North End

Regina Pizzeria

The original North End pizza oven, firing since 1926.

11 1/2 Thacher St, Boston, MA 02113

Café · North End

Mike's Pastry

The famous cannoli in the white box tied with string.

300 Hanover St, Boston, MA 02113

Café · North End

Modern Pastry

The locals' cannoli, filled to order, across the street from Mike's. Try both.

257 Hanover St, Boston, MA 02113

Restaurant · Faneuil Hall

Quincy Market

A long food hall behind Faneuil Hall. Clam chowder in a bread bowl, eaten on the steps.

4 S Market St, Boston, MA 02109

Restaurant · Seaport

Row 34

A working-class oyster bar in the Seaport, with a great beer list.

383 Congress St, Boston, MA 02210

Restaurant · South End

Toro

Busy, delicious Spanish tapas. Order the grilled corn with aioli and cheese.

1704 Washington St, Boston, MA 02118

Restaurant · South End

Myers + Chang

A playful Asian diner from the team behind Flour. The dumplings and the tea-smoked ribs.

1145 Washington St, Boston, MA 02118

Café · South End

Flour Bakery + Cafe

Joanne Chang's bakery. The sticky buns are the thing to order.

1595 Washington St, Boston, MA 02118

Café · Beacon Hill

Tatte Bakery & Cafe

A bright bakery on Charles Street. Breakfast before a Beacon Hill walk.

70 Charles St, Boston, MA 02114

Restaurant · East Boston

Santarpio's Pizza

No-frills pizza and grilled lamb skewers in East Boston since 1903.

111 Chelsea St, Boston, MA 02128

Restaurant · South Boston

Sullivan's Castle Island

Hot dogs and fried clams by the water since 1951. Open from late February to November.

2080 William J Day Blvd, Boston, MA 02127

Bar · Faneuil Hall

Bell in Hand Tavern

Founded in 1795 by the town crier, and still pouring on the Freedom Trail.

45 Union St, Boston, MA 02108

Bar · Fenway

Bleacher Bar

A bar under Fenway's bleachers, with a window right onto center field.

82A Lansdowne St, Boston, MA 02215

Museum · North End

Paul Revere House

Downtown Boston's oldest house, built around 1680, where Revere lived when he made his ride.

19 North Square, Boston, MA 02113

Attraction · North End

Old North Church

Where the two lanterns hung in 1775. The behind-the-scenes tour goes into the crypt.

193 Salem St, Boston, MA 02113

Attraction · Faneuil Hall

Faneuil Hall

A 1742 meeting hall where colonists argued for independence. Rangers give free talks upstairs.

1 Faneuil Hall Sq, Boston, MA 02109

Attraction · Beacon Hill

Massachusetts State House

The gold-domed State House. Free tours on weekdays.

24 Beacon St, Boston, MA 02133

Museum · Charlestown

USS Constitution Museum

Board Old Ironsides, the oldest commissioned warship afloat, then see the museum next door.

Charlestown Navy Yard, Boston, MA 02129

Museum · Fenway

Museum of Fine Arts, Boston

One of the great American museums. Don't miss the Sargent murals over the rotunda.

465 Huntington Ave, Boston, MA 02115

Museum · Fenway

Isabella Stewart Gardner Museum

A Venetian palace built around a flowering courtyard, with the empty frames of the 1990 heist.

25 Evans Way, Boston, MA 02115

Attraction · Back Bay

Boston Public Library

The 1895 McKim Building and its green-lamped Bates Hall. Free art and architecture tours.

700 Boylston St, Boston, MA 02116

Attraction · Back Bay

Trinity Church

H.H. Richardson's 1877 church on Copley Square, glowing inside with murals and stained glass.

206 Clarendon St, Boston, MA 02116

Attraction · Fenway

Fenway Park

The oldest ballpark in the majors, opened in 1912. Take the tour if there's no game.

4 Jersey St, Boston, MA 02215

Attraction · Waterfront

New England Aquarium

A four-story ocean tank with sea turtles, and whale-watching boats leaving from the wharf.

1 Central Wharf, Boston, MA 02110

Museum · Dorchester

John F. Kennedy Presidential Library and Museum

JFK's life and presidency in an I.M. Pei building on the harbor.

Columbia Point, Boston, MA 02125

Museum · Seaport

Institute of Contemporary Art

Contemporary art in a building cantilevered over the harbor. The view from the Mediatheque is a highlight.

25 Harbor Shore Dr, Boston, MA 02210

Attraction · Cambridge

Harvard Yard

Walk the yard, rub John Harvard's shoe, then browse the bookshops of Harvard Square.

Harvard Yard, Cambridge, MA 02138

Museum · Cambridge

MIT Museum

Robots, holograms and kinetic sculpture, in a new building in Kendall Square.

314 Main St, Cambridge, MA 02142

Outside

Park · Downtown

Boston Common

America's oldest public park, and the start of the Freedom Trail. Follow the red brick line.

139 Tremont St, Boston, MA 02111

Park · Back Bay

Boston Public Garden

Swan boats on the lagoon in summer, and the Make Way for Ducklings statues all year.

Charles St, Boston, MA 02116

Park · Back Bay

Charles River Esplanade

A long riverside path with sailboats and the Hatch Shell. Lovely at sunset.

Charles River Esplanade, Boston, MA 02114

Park · Waterfront

Rose Kennedy Greenway

A park built over the old elevated highway, with fountains, a carousel and food trucks.

Rose Kennedy Greenway, Boston, MA 02110

Park · Jamaica Plain

Arnold Arboretum

Harvard's free arboretum. Climb Peters Hill for a view of the skyline, and come in May for the lilacs.

125 Arborway, Boston, MA 02130

Park · South Boston

Castle Island

A loop around Fort Independence, with planes landing overhead. Pair it with Sullivan's.

2010 William J Day Blvd, Boston, MA 02127
