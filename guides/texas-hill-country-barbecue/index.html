Central Texas Barbecue from Lockhart to Llano

A barbecue trail out of Austin through Taylor, Elgin, Lexington, Lockhart, Luling, Driftwood and Llano, from century-old meat markets to new pits, with a few places to sleep along the way.

Texas · 20 places · Picked by friends of Placemoir · Checked November 14, 2026

Austin · the starting point

Restaurant · Austin

Franklin Barbecue

The brisket that set off the modern Austin barbecue boom. The line forms well before opening and the meat often sells out, so plan the morning around it or look into preorders on the official site.

900 E 11th St, Austin, TX 78702

Restaurant · Austin

la Barbecue

An East Austin pit with roots in the Mueller barbecue family of Taylor. Peppery brisket, pork ribs and house sausage, served until it runs out.

2401 E Cesar Chavez St, Austin, TX 78702

Restaurant · Austin

LeRoy and Lewis Barbecue

South Austin barbecue that treats the sides and the less familiar cuts as seriously as the brisket. Beef cheeks made its name, and the vegetables get real smoke too.

5621 Emerald Forest Dr, Austin, TX 78745

Restaurant · Austin

InterStellar BBQ

A strip-mall counter in Northwest Austin that turns out some of the most precise barbecue in the state. Convenient on the way out toward Llano or Spicewood.

12233 Ranch Rd 620 N, Suite 105, Austin, TX 78750

Taylor, Elgin and Lexington · 25 to 55 mi northeast of Austin

Restaurant · Taylor

Louie Mueller Barbecue

Run by the same family since the 1940s in a room whose walls have gone dark from decades of smoke. Order at the counter, then take a look at the pits. The big beef rib is the classic order.

206 W 2nd St, Taylor, TX 76574

Restaurant · Elgin

Southside Market & Barbeque

Elgin has long been called the sausage capital of Texas, and Southside has been making its hot links since 1882. Eat in the big hall or buy links at the meat counter to take home.

1212 US Highway 290 W, Elgin, TX 78621

Restaurant · Lexington

Snow's BBQ

A small-town pit open on Saturday mornings only, famous for its longtime pitmaster Tootsie Tomanetz. Get there early: the line starts at dawn and the meat is often gone by late morning. Check the official site before driving out.

516 Main St, Lexington, TX 78947

Lockhart · 30 mi south of Austin

Restaurant · Lockhart

Kreuz Market

A huge barn of a meat market with roots going back to 1900. Order by the pound in the pit room and carry it out on butcher paper. The salt-and-pepper brisket and the house sausage are the classic orders.

619 N Colorado St, Lockhart, TX 78644

Restaurant · Lockhart

Smitty's Market

The original Kreuz building, where you walk through a smoky back hall past open fire pits to order. Even if you only stop for a sausage ring, come for the room.

208 S Commerce St, Lockhart, TX 78644

Restaurant · Lockhart

Black's Barbecue

One family has run these pits since 1932. Known for oversized beef ribs and a long cafeteria line of sides. The walls are covered in old photos.

215 N Main St, Lockhart, TX 78644

Restaurant · Lockhart

Chisholm Trail Bar-B-Q

The everyday local spot on the south side of town, cafeteria style with plenty of sides. Easier on the wallet and the clock than the famous names.

1323 S Colorado St, Lockhart, TX 78644

Attraction · Lockhart

Caldwell County Courthouse

An ornate 1890s courthouse at the center of a square lined with shops, coffee and antiques. A good place to walk off lunch between pits.

110 S Main St, Lockhart, TX 78644

Park · Lockhart

Lockhart State Park

A small state park just south of town with a creek, short trails, a nine-hole golf course and a compact campground. Reserve sites through Texas Parks and Wildlife.

2012 State Park Rd, Lockhart, TX 78644

Luling · 15 mi south of Lockhart

Restaurant · Luling

City Market

Order meat in the glassed-in pit room at the back, then sides and drinks up front. Pork ribs, sausage and the house mustard sauce are the reasons to come.

633 E Davis St, Luling, TX 78648

Park · Ottine

Palmetto State Park

A few miles south of Luling, dwarf palmettos and swampy trails along the San Marcos River feel nothing like the rest of Central Texas. Has a campground and river paddling.

78 Park Road 11 S, Gonzales, TX 78629

Driftwood and Spicewood · 25 to 35 mi west of Austin

Restaurant · Driftwood

The Salt Lick BBQ

A big open pit under a stone building on a Hill Country ranch, with family-style platters at picnic tables. More about the setting and the afternoon than the brisket. The Driftwood location is BYOB; check the official site for details.

18300 FM 1826, Driftwood, TX 78619

Hotel · Dripping Springs

Camp Lucy

A ranch resort near Dripping Springs with rooms and cottages under old live oaks, about fifteen minutes from the Salt Lick. The splurge stay on this list.

3509 Creek Rd, Dripping Springs, TX 78620

Restaurant · Spicewood

Opie's Barbecue

Pick meat straight from the pit outside, Llano style, then grab sides inside. The sweet-and-spicy baby back ribs and tater tot casserole are the usual orders. An easy stop on the way to Llano.

9504 E State Highway 71, Spicewood, TX 78669

Llano · 75 mi northwest of Austin

Restaurant · Llano

Cooper's Old Time Pit Bar-B-Que

The original Cooper's. Point at what you want from the open mesquite pits out front, then eat at long communal tables inside. The thick pork chop is the signature, and the beans are free.

604 W Young St, Llano, TX 78643

Hotel · Llano

Dabbs Hotel

A 1907 railroad hotel in Llano's old railyard district, now a small bed-and-breakfast with six suites and a backyard that runs down to the river. A short walk to the courthouse square.

112 E Burnet St, Llano, TX 78643
