A week in Chicago

Seven days of architecture by boat, deep dish and Italian beef, the Art Institute and the lakefront, blues bars and a game at Wrigley.

43 places · Picked by friends of Placemoir · Checked November 8, 2026

Stay

Hotel · Gold Coast

The Drake Hotel

A 1920 grand hotel at the top of the Magnificent Mile, steps from Oak Street Beach. Tea in the Palm Court.

140 E Walton Pl, Chicago, IL 60611

Hotel · The Loop

Chicago Athletic Association

An 1893 Venetian Gothic men's club turned hotel, facing Millennium Park. The rooftop bar has the view.

12 S Michigan Ave, Chicago, IL 60603

Hotel · River North

The Langham, Chicago

The splurge, in a Mies van der Rohe tower on the river. Big rooms and a beautiful pool.

330 N Wabash Ave, Chicago, IL 60611

Hotel · River North

Freehand Chicago

Private rooms and shared bunks, with an excellent cocktail bar, Broken Shaker. The budget pick.

19 E Ohio St, Chicago, IL 60611

Eat

Restaurant · River North

Portillo's

Chicago's Italian beef and hot dogs in one place. Order the beef dipped, with hot peppers, and a chocolate cake shake.

100 W Ontario St, Chicago, IL 60654

Restaurant · River North

Mr. Beef on Orleans

The tiny beef stand that inspired The Bear. Eat it at the counter, dripping.

666 N Orleans St, Chicago, IL 60654

Restaurant · River North

Lou Malnati's Pizzeria

Deep dish with a buttery crust. Order ahead; it takes 45 minutes.

439 N Wells St, Chicago, IL 60654

Restaurant · Lincoln Park

Pequod's Pizza

Pan pizza with a burnt-cheese crust around the edge. The locals' favorite.

2207 N Clybourn Ave, Chicago, IL 60614

Restaurant · West Loop

Au Cheval

A diner known for one of the best burgers in America. Put your name in and have a drink nearby.

800 W Randolph St, Chicago, IL 60607

Restaurant · West Loop

Girl & the Goat

Stephanie Izard's bold, shareable plates on Restaurant Row. Book ahead.

809 W Randolph St, Chicago, IL 60607

Restaurant · River North

Frontera Grill

Rick Bayless's regional Mexican cooking. Come for the moles and the margaritas.

445 N Clark St, Chicago, IL 60654

Restaurant · Ukrainian Village

Kasama

A Filipino bakery by day, with lines for the ube croissant, and a Michelin-starred tasting menu by night.

1001 N Winchester Ave, Chicago, IL 60622

Restaurant · Lincoln Park

Alinea

The once-in-a-lifetime tasting menu, all theater and surprise. Tickets go on sale months ahead.

1723 N Halsted St, Chicago, IL 60614

Restaurant · West Loop

Lou Mitchell's

Breakfast since 1923, at the start of Route 66. Free doughnut holes while you wait.

565 W Jackson Blvd, Chicago, IL 60661

Restaurant · South Loop

Manny's Cafeteria & Delicatessen

A cafeteria deli where politicians eat corned beef and potato pancakes.

1141 S Jefferson St, Chicago, IL 60607

Restaurant · Norwood Park

Superdawg Drive-In

A 1948 drive-in with giant hot dog mascots on the roof. Carhops bring it to your window.

6363 N Milwaukee Ave, Chicago, IL 60646

Café · The Loop

Intelligentsia Coffee

The Chicago roaster's flagship coffee bar, across from Millennium Park.

53 E Randolph St, Chicago, IL 60601

Bar · Uptown

The Green Mill

Al Capone's jazz club, still swinging. Sunday night is the original poetry slam.

4802 N Broadway, Chicago, IL 60640

Bar · Lincoln Park

Kingston Mines

Two stages of live blues every night until late.

2548 N Halsted St, Chicago, IL 60614

Bar · Wicker Park

The Violet Hour

A hidden, candlelit cocktail bar behind a mural-covered wall. No phones at the bar.

1520 N Damen Ave, Chicago, IL 60622

Bar · Streeterville

Billy Goat Tavern

Cheezborgers under Michigan Avenue, made famous by Saturday Night Live and the Cubs' curse.

430 N Michigan Ave, Chicago, IL 60611

See

Museum · The Loop

Art Institute of Chicago

Nighthawks, American Gothic and a huge Impressionist collection. Give it at least half a day.

111 S Michigan Ave, Chicago, IL 60603

Attraction · The Loop

Chicago Architecture Center

Take the architecture river cruise that leaves from the dock below. The best 90 minutes in the city.

111 E Wacker Dr, Chicago, IL 60601

Attraction · The Loop

Skydeck Chicago

Step out onto the glass Ledge, 103 floors up in Willis Tower.

233 S Wacker Dr, Chicago, IL 60606

Museum · Museum Campus

Field Museum

Natural history, and Máximo the titanosaur. Combine it with the Shedd next door.

1400 S DuSable Lake Shore Dr, Chicago, IL 60605

Attraction · Museum Campus

Shedd Aquarium

Belugas, sea otters and a Caribbean reef, with the lake outside the windows.

1200 S DuSable Lake Shore Dr, Chicago, IL 60605

Museum · Museum Campus

Adler Planetarium

America's first planetarium, at the end of a point with the best skyline view in Chicago.

1300 S DuSable Lake Shore Dr, Chicago, IL 60605

Museum · Hyde Park

Museum of Science and Industry

A real U-boat, a coal mine and a model railroad, in the last building of the 1893 World's Fair.

5700 S DuSable Lake Shore Dr, Chicago, IL 60637

Museum · Hyde Park

Frederick C. Robie House

Frank Lloyd Wright's Prairie-style masterpiece. Pair it with the science museum.

5757 S Woodlawn Ave, Chicago, IL 60637

Museum · Oak Park

Frank Lloyd Wright Home and Studio

Where Wright lived and worked for twenty years. Walk the street afterwards to see his early houses.

951 Chicago Ave, Oak Park, IL 60302

Attraction · The Loop

Chicago Cultural Center

Free, with the world's largest Tiffany glass dome. Duck in on the way to Millennium Park.

78 E Washington St, Chicago, IL 60602

Museum · Streeterville

Museum of Contemporary Art Chicago

Bold contemporary art near the lake, and free for Illinois residents on Tuesdays.

220 E Chicago Ave, Chicago, IL 60611

Attraction · Wrigleyville

Wrigley Field

Ivy walls and a hand-turned scoreboard since 1914. A day game is the classic.

1060 W Addison St, Chicago, IL 60613

Attraction · Streeterville

Navy Pier

A ride on the Centennial Wheel, and fireworks over the lake on summer nights.

600 E Grand Ave, Chicago, IL 60611

Attraction · Streeterville

360 Chicago

The 94th-floor view up the lakeshore, from the old Hancock tower. Have a drink at sunset.

875 N Michigan Ave, Chicago, IL 60611

Outside

Park · The Loop

Millennium Park

The Bean, the Crown Fountain and free summer concerts at the Pritzker Pavilion.

201 E Randolph St, Chicago, IL 60602

Park · The Loop

Maggie Daley Park

A playful park with a climbing wall and an ice-skating ribbon in winter.

337 E Randolph St, Chicago, IL 60601

Park · The Loop

Chicago Riverwalk

A walk along the river under the drawbridges, with wine bars and kayaks along the way.

Chicago Riverwalk, Chicago, IL 60601

Park · Lincoln Park

Lincoln Park Zoo

A free zoo in the city's biggest park, open every day of the year.

2400 N Cannon Dr, Chicago, IL 60614

Park · Lincoln Park

Lincoln Park Conservatory

A free Victorian glasshouse full of palms and orchids, next to the zoo.

2391 N Stockton Dr, Chicago, IL 60614

Park · Lincoln Park

North Avenue Beach

Sand, volleyball and skyline views. Rent a bike and ride the Lakefront Trail.

1601 N Jean-Baptiste Pointe DuSable Lake Shore Dr, Chicago, IL 60611

Park · Wicker Park

The 606

A 2.7-mile elevated trail on an old railway through Wicker Park and Bucktown.

The 606, Chicago, IL 60647

Park · Garfield Park

Garfield Park Conservatory

One of the biggest conservatories in the country, and free. A warm escape in winter.

300 N Central Park Ave, Chicago, IL 60624
