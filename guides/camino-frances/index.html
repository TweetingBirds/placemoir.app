The Camino Francés from Saint-Jean-Pied-de-Port to Santiago de Compostela

The classic 780 km pilgrim route across northern Spain in 33 stages, from the Pyrenees over the meseta to Galicia, with a place to sleep at the end of each day and the sights and meals worth stopping for.

France, Navarre, La Rioja, Castile and León, Galicia · 90 places · Picked by friends of Placemoir · Checked November 14, 2026

Stage 1 · Saint-Jean-Pied-de-Port to Roncesvalles · 25 km

Attraction · Saint-Jean-Pied-de-Port

1. Accueil des Pèlerins

The volunteer pilgrim office on the cobbled main street. Pick up a credential and the first stamp, and ask about conditions on the Route Napoléon before setting out, since the high pass can close in bad weather.

39 Rue de la Citadelle, 64220 Saint-Jean-Pied-de-Port, France

Hotel · Orisson

2. Refuge Orisson

A mountain refuge about 8 km up the climb, for those who want to split the Pyrenees crossing in two. Shared dinner on a terrace with a long view back over the valley. Open roughly April to mid-October; check the official site for booking.

RD 428, 64220 Uhart-Cize

Hotel · Roncesvalles

3. Albergue de Roncesvalles

The big pilgrim hostel in the restored monastery buildings, run by the Colegiata. Bunks are in tidy cubicles and nearly everyone starting in Saint-Jean sleeps here the first night.

Calle Única s/n, 31650 Roncesvalles, Navarra

Attraction · Roncesvalles

4. Real Colegiata de Santa María de Roncesvalles

A Gothic church and cloister that have sheltered pilgrims for about nine centuries. The evening pilgrim Mass with a blessing in several languages is a good way to end the first day.

Calle Única s/n, 31650 Roncesvalles, Navarra

Stage 2 · Roncesvalles to Zubiri · 22 km

Hotel · Burguete

5. Hostal Burguete

An old family inn on the main street of Burguete, 3 km past Roncesvalles, where Hemingway stayed in the 1920s and wrote it into The Sun Also Rises. A comfortable private room after the first night in a dorm.

Calle de San Nikolas, 71, 31640 Auritz, Navarra

Attraction · Zubiri

6. Puente de la Rabia

The medieval bridge over the Arga that leads into Zubiri. Local lore said walking an animal around its central pillar cured rabies. Most pilgrims cool their feet in the river below.

Calle del Puente de la Rabia, 31630 Esteribar, Navarra

Stage 3 · Zubiri to Pamplona · 21 km

Hotel · Pamplona

7. Albergue Jesús y María

The municipal pilgrim hostel inside a former Jesuit church in the old town, a few steps from the cathedral. Big, well run and central. It can close for stretches of the year, notably around San Fermín in July, so check before you go.

Calle Compañía 4, 31001 Pamplona, Navarra

Café · Pamplona

8. Café Iruña

A grand 1888 café on Plaza del Castillo with mirrors, marble and a long bar. Stop for coffee or pintxos and look for the Hemingway statue in the side room.

Plaza del Castillo 44, 31001 Pamplona, Navarra

Attraction · Pamplona

9. Catedral de Pamplona

Behind a neoclassical front is a Gothic church with one of the finest cloisters in Spain. The visit includes the old kitchen and a museum of Navarrese religious art.

Calle Dormitalería 1, 31001 Pamplona, Navarra

Stage 4 · Pamplona to Puente la Reina · 24 km

Park · Alto del Perdón

10. Alto del Perdón

The windmill ridge about 13 km out of Pamplona, where iron silhouettes of medieval pilgrims on foot and horseback line the crest. Take the descent slowly; the loose stones are hard on knees.

Calle San Andrés, 12, 31190 Zariquiegui, Navarra

Attraction · Muruzábal

11. Santa María de Eunate

An octagonal Romanesque church alone in the fields, ringed by an arcade. It is a short detour off the main route before Obanos; ask locally whether it is open before you go.

Carretera NA-601 s/n, 31152 Muruzábal, Navarra

Hotel · Puente la Reina

12. Albergue Padres Reparadores

A parish-style hostel run by a religious order at the entrance to town, beside the Church of the Crucifix. A garden out back for drying laundry and resting feet.

Calle Crucifijo 1, 31100 Puente la Reina, Navarra

Attraction · Puente la Reina

13. Puente Románico de Puente la Reina

The six-arched 11th-century bridge built for pilgrims over the Arga, at the end of the long main street. Cross it at dusk, then look back from the riverbank.

Calle Mayor, 31100 Puente la Reina, Navarra

Stage 5 · Puente la Reina to Estella · 22 km

Attraction · Estella

14. Iglesia de San Pedro de la Rúa

A Romanesque church up a long stair above the Rúa, with a carved doorway and a partly surviving cloister. Visits are often by guided tour from the tourist office.

Calle San Pedro de la Rúa, 31200 Estella, Navarra

Hotel · Estella

15. Albergue Municipal de Peregrinos de Estella

The public hostel on the old pilgrim street, right on the route as you come into town and close to San Pedro de la Rúa.

Calle La Rúa 50, 31200 Estella, Navarra

Stage 6 · Estella to Los Arcos · 21 km

Attraction · Ayegui

16. Fuente del Vino de Irache

A winery's free wine tap set into a wall beside the trail just past Estella, with a water tap next to it. A small glass is the custom, not a bottle refill.

Camino de Santiago, 31240 Ayegui, Navarra

Attraction · Los Arcos

17. Iglesia de Santa María de Los Arcos

A plain outside hides a lavish Baroque interior and a quiet Gothic cloister. Many pilgrims time their evening around the Mass and blessing.

Plaza Santa María, 31210 Los Arcos, Navarra

Hotel · Los Arcos

18. Albergue Isaac Santiago

The municipal hostel at the far edge of Los Arcos, run by volunteers. A walled patio for stretching out after the long, dry walk from Villamayor.

Calle San Lázaro s/n, 31210 Los Arcos, Navarra

Stage 7 · Los Arcos to Logroño · 28 km

Hotel · Logroño

19. Albergue Municipal de Peregrinos de Logroño

The city's public hostel in the old quarter, a short walk from the tapas streets. A shaded courtyard and plenty of beds.

Rúa Vieja 32, 26001 Logroño, La Rioja

Attraction · Logroño

20. Calle del Laurel

A narrow old-town street packed with small bars, each known for one pincho, from grilled mushrooms to fried peppers, with a glass of Rioja. Go bar to bar rather than settling in one.

Calle del Laurel, 26001 Logroño, La Rioja

Stage 8 · Logroño to Nájera · 29 km

Attraction · Navarrete

21. Iglesia de la Asunción de Navarrete

A 16th-century church with an enormous gilded Baroque altarpiece, in a hill town known for pottery. A good rest stop about 12 km into the day.

Calle La Cuesta, 26370 Navarrete, La Rioja

Attraction · Nájera

22. Monasterio de Santa María la Real

Built into the red cliff at the edge of town, with royal tombs of Navarre, a carved choir and a lacy Gothic cloister. The cave where the church was founded is at the back.

Plaza Santa María 1, 26300 Nájera, La Rioja

Hotel · Nájera

23. Albergue Municipal de Nájera

A large public hostel run by volunteers just across the river in the old town, steps from the monastery.

Plaza de Santiago s/n, 26300 Nájera, La Rioja

Stage 9 · Nájera to Santo Domingo de la Calzada · 21 km

Attraction · Santo Domingo de la Calzada

24. Catedral de Santo Domingo de la Calzada

A live rooster and hen are kept in an ornate coop inside the cathedral, recalling the legend of the hanged pilgrim. Tradition says it is good luck if the rooster crows while you are inside.

Plaza del Santo 4, 26250 Santo Domingo de la Calzada, La Rioja

Hotel · Santo Domingo de la Calzada

25. Parador de Santo Domingo de la Calzada

A state-run hotel in the medieval pilgrim hospital founded by the saint himself, facing the cathedral. Stone arches in the lobby and a quiet night if you want a break from bunks.

Plaza del Santo 3, 26250 Santo Domingo de la Calzada, La Rioja

Hotel · Santo Domingo de la Calzada

26. Albergue Casa del Santo

The confraternity's hostel on the main street, with a big kitchen and a yard where some of the cathedral's chickens are kept between shifts.

Calle Mayor 42, 26250 Santo Domingo de la Calzada, La Rioja

Stage 10 · Santo Domingo de la Calzada to Belorado · 23 km

Hotel · Grañón

27. Albergue Parroquial San Juan Bautista

Pilgrims sleep on mats in the loft of the parish church, cook and eat together, and pay by donation. No reservations. A night many remember long after the walk.

Plaza de la Iglesia, 26259 Grañón, La Rioja

Hotel · Belorado

28. Albergue Cuatro Cantones

A family-run private hostel on the way into Belorado with a pool, a garden and a dining room serving pilgrim dinners.

Calle Hipólito López Bernal 10, 09250 Belorado, Burgos

Stage 11 · Belorado to San Juan de Ortega · 24 km

Hotel · Villafranca Montes de Oca

29. Hotel San Antón Abad

A hotel in a 14th-century pilgrim hospital at the foot of the Oca hills, with private rooms and a separate pilgrim dorm. A good place to rest or stop for the night before the climb through the oak and pine forest.

Calle Hospital 4, 09257 Villafranca Montes de Oca, Burgos

Attraction · San Juan de Ortega

30. Monasterio de San Juan de Ortega

A monastery church in a clearing in the oak forest, with the saint's tomb and a capital lit by the sun at the equinoxes. The pilgrim hostel is in the old monastery buildings next door.

Calle de la Iglesia, 09199 San Juan de Ortega, Burgos

Stage 12 · San Juan de Ortega to Burgos · 26 km

Hotel · Burgos

31. Albergue Municipal Casa del Cubo

The city's public hostel in a restored town house right behind the cathedral. Large, modern and as central as it gets.

Calle Fernán González 28, 09003 Burgos

Attraction · Burgos

32. Catedral de Burgos

One of the great Gothic cathedrals of Europe, with spires that look like stone lace and chapels that could each fill an afternoon. Pilgrims with a credential can ask about a reduced ticket.

Plaza de Santa María, 09003 Burgos

Bar · Burgos

33. Cervecería Morito

A crowded tapas bar near the cathedral, known for generous plates of morcilla, croquetas and grilled mushrooms. Expect to wait for a spot at the bar.

Calle Diego Porcelos 1, 09003 Burgos

Stage 13 · Burgos to Hornillos del Camino · 21 km

Attraction · Rabé de las Calzadas

34. Ermita de Nuestra Señora de Monasterio

A small chapel at the end of Rabé where a local woman has long offered pilgrims a blessing and a medal. The open meseta begins just beyond.

Camino de Santiago, 09130 Rabé de las Calzadas, Burgos

Hotel · Hornillos del Camino

35. Albergue Municipal de Hornillos del Camino

A small public hostel beside the church in a one-street village on the meseta. Fills early, so carry on to the private hostels in the same street if it is full.

Plaza de la Iglesia s/n, 09230 Hornillos del Camino, Burgos

Stage 14 · Hornillos del Camino to Castrojeriz · 20 km

Attraction · Castrojeriz

36. Ruinas del Convento de San Antón

The road runs straight under the Gothic arches of a ruined 14th-century monastery, once a hospital for pilgrims. Volunteers run a simple candlelit hostel inside in the warmer months.

Carretera BU-P-4013, 09110 Castrojeriz, Burgos

Attraction · Castrojeriz

37. Castillo de Castrojeriz

The ruined castle on the hill above town. A steep half-hour climb in the evening gives the best view over the meseta and tomorrow's ridge, Alto de Mostelares.

Subida al Castillo, 09110 Castrojeriz, Burgos

Hotel · Castrojeriz

38. Albergue de Peregrinos San Esteban

The municipal hostel in an old building on the main square, run on donations. Basic and friendly, in the middle of the long single street of Castrojeriz.

Plaza Mayor 16, 09110 Castrojeriz, Burgos

Stage 15 · Castrojeriz to Frómista · 25 km

Attraction · Itero del Castillo

39. Ermita de San Nicolás

A 13th-century chapel by the bridge over the Pisuerga, run by an Italian confraternity as a summer-only hostel with no electricity, where hosts wash pilgrims' feet before dinner.

Puente Fitero, 09107 Itero del Castillo, Burgos

Hotel · Boadilla del Camino

40. Albergue En El Camino

A private hostel with a big lawn, a pool and art on the walls, beside the old stone justice column in Boadilla. A good place to stop short of Frómista.

Calle Rosario 1, 34468 Boadilla del Camino, Palencia

Attraction · Frómista

41. Iglesia de San Martín de Frómista

A small, almost perfect Romanesque church from the 11th century. Walk around the outside to see more than 300 carved corbels under the eaves.

Plaza de San Martín, 34440 Frómista, Palencia

Stage 16 · Frómista to Carrión de los Condes · 19 km

Attraction · Villalcázar de Sirga

42. Iglesia de Santa María la Blanca

A fortress-like Templar church in a tiny village, with painted royal tombs and a Virgin celebrated for miracles in medieval song.

Plaza Mayor, 34449 Villalcázar de Sirga, Palencia

Hotel · Carrión de los Condes

43. Hotel Real Monasterio de San Zoilo

A hotel in a Benedictine monastery across the river from Carrión, with a Renaissance cloister open to visitors. A quiet, comfortable splurge on the meseta.

Calle Obispo Soto Vizoso s/n, 34120 Carrión de los Condes, Palencia

Hotel · Carrión de los Condes

44. Albergue Parroquial de Santa María

The parish hostel run by the Augustinian sisters, known for an evening of singing and conversation with pilgrims before Mass.

Calle Clérigo Pastor 2, 34120 Carrión de los Condes, Palencia

Stage 17 · Carrión de los Condes to Terradillos de los Templarios · 27 km

Hotel · Terradillos de los Templarios

45. Albergue Jacques de Molay

A family-run hostel in the village center with dorms, private rooms and a set dinner. Before Calzadilla de la Cueza there is a 17 km stretch with no services, so carry water.

Calle Iglesia 18, 34349 Terradillos de los Templarios, Palencia

Hotel · Terradillos de los Templarios

46. Albergue Los Templarios

A large brick hostel just before the village, with a lawn, private rooms and a restaurant. The second solid choice in this one-street hamlet.

Camino Francés, 34349 Lagartos, Palencia

Stage 18 · Terradillos de los Templarios to El Burgo Ranero · 30 km

Attraction · Sahagún

47. Arco de San Benito

A Baroque gate that is nearly all that survives of the once-powerful Benedictine monastery of Sahagún. The brick Mudéjar churches nearby are the town's real character.

Avenida Doctores Bermejo y Calderón, 24320 Sahagún, León

Museum · Sahagún

48. Santuario de la Peregrina

A restored Franciscan convent on the hill, with Mudéjar plasterwork and a museum. Ask here for the certificate marking the halfway point of the Camino.

Calle Arco de San Francisco, 24320 Sahagún, León

Hotel · El Burgo Ranero

49. Albergue Municipal Domenico Laffi

A small adobe hostel built in the traditional style, named for a 17th-century Italian pilgrim who wrote about the route. Donation-based and run by volunteers.

Calle de la Carretera, 11, 24343 El Burgo Ranero, León

Stage 19 · El Burgo Ranero to Mansilla de las Mulas · 19 km

Museum · Mansilla de las Mulas

50. Museo de los Pueblos Leoneses

An ethnographic museum in an old Augustinian convent, with costume, farm tools and village crafts from across León province. A good rest stop on an easy day.

Calle San Agustín, 24210 Mansilla de las Mulas, León

Hotel · Mansilla de las Mulas

51. Albergue Municipal de Mansilla de las Mulas

The public hostel inside the medieval walls, with a courtyard full of drying laundry. A short walk from the stretch of town wall above the river.

Calle del Puente 5, 24210 Mansilla de las Mulas, León

Stage 20 · Mansilla de las Mulas to León · 19 km

Attraction · León

52. Catedral de León

A Gothic cathedral that is more glass than wall, with nearly 1,800 square meters of medieval stained glass. Visit on a sunny morning when the color falls across the floor.

Plaza Regla, 3, 24003 León

Museum · León

53. Real Colegiata de San Isidoro

The Romanesque royal church of León, where the Pantheon of Kings has 12th-century painted vaults in almost perfect condition. Some call it the Sistine Chapel of Romanesque art.

Plaza de San Isidoro 4, 24003 León

Hotel · León

54. Albergue de las Benedictinas Santa María de Carbajal

A convent hostel run by Benedictine nuns in the old town, a few minutes' walk from the cathedral. Pilgrims are invited to the sisters' evening prayer and blessing.

Plaza de Santa María del Camino 7, 24003 León

Hotel · León

55. Parador de León

The vast Renaissance convent of San Marcos, once a pilgrim hospital and now a state hotel with a famous plateresque facade. Even if you do not stay, look into the cloister.

Plaza de San Marcos, 7, 24008 León

Attraction · León

56. Barrio Húmedo

The old-town knot of streets around Plaza de San Martín, packed with bars that still serve a free tapa with each drink. Try morcilla and cecina, the cured beef of León.

Plaza de San Martín, 24003 León

Stage 21 · León to Hospital de Órbigo · 32 km

Attraction · La Virgen del Camino

57. Basílica de la Virgen del Camino

A striking 1960s sanctuary on the edge of León, with the twelve apostles and the Virgin cast in bronze across its tall front. An unexpected modern church on an old road.

Avenida Astroga, 94, 24198 Virgen Camino, León

Attraction · Hospital de Órbigo

58. Puente de Órbigo

One of the longest medieval bridges in Spain, about 20 arches, where a knight held a famous jousting challenge in 1434. A tournament is still staged in the fields below each summer.

Puente de Órbigo, 24286 Hospital de Órbigo, León

Hotel · Hospital de Órbigo

59. Albergue Parroquial Karl Leisner

The parish hostel in an old house with a leafy courtyard, on the main street just past the bridge.

Calle Álvarez Vega 32, 24286 Hospital de Órbigo, León

Stage 22 · Hospital de Órbigo to Astorga · 17 km

Museum · Astorga

60. Palacio de Gaudí

A fairytale bishop's palace by Antoni Gaudí, now the Museum of the Ways, with Camino history inside. One of only three Gaudí buildings outside Catalonia.

Plaza Eduardo de Castro 15, 24700 Astorga, León

Hotel · Astorga

61. Albergue Siervas de María

The big association-run hostel at the top of the steep climb into Astorga, right on the route. A terrace with a view toward the Montes de León.

Plaza de San Francisco 3, 24700 Astorga, León

Stage 23 · Astorga to Rabanal del Camino · 20 km

Restaurant · Castrillo de los Polvazares

62. Hostería Cuca la Vaina

A short detour to a stone village built by muleteers, where this inn serves cocido maragato, a stew eaten backward, meats first and soup last. Plan on a big lunch and a short afternoon.

Calle la Magdalena, 10, 24718 Castrillo de los Polvazares, León

Hotel · Rabanal del Camino

63. Refugio Gaucelmo

A volunteer-run hostel in a restored priest's house with a walled garden, set up by the UK-based Confraternity of Saint James. Open April to October, with afternoon tea and the monks' sung prayers at the church next door.

Lugar Rabanal Camino, 21D, 24722 Santa Colomba de Somoza, León

Stage 24 · Rabanal del Camino to Molinaseca · 25 km

Attraction · Foncebadón

64. Cruz de Ferro

A small iron cross on a tall wooden pole near the highest point of the Camino, on a mound of stones carried by pilgrims from home. Bring one and leave it with whatever it stands for.

LE-142, 24722 Santa Colomba de Somoza, León

Attraction · Molinaseca

65. Puente de los Peregrinos

A medieval bridge over the Meruelo into Molinaseca, with a river pool below where tired walkers soak after the long descent from the mountains.

Calle Real, 24413 Molinaseca, León

Hotel · Molinaseca

66. Hostal El Palacio

A small family hostal at the end of the bridge, with a terrace restaurant over the river. Private rooms for a night off from bunks.

Calle El Palacio 19, 24413 Molinaseca, León

Stage 25 · Molinaseca to Villafranca del Bierzo · 31 km

Museum · Ponferrada

67. Castillo de los Templarios

A great Templar castle in the middle of Ponferrada, with towers, walls and a library of facsimile medieval books inside. Give it an hour on the way through.

Avenida del Castillo, 131–143, 24401 Ponferrada, León

Attraction · Villafranca del Bierzo

68. Iglesia de Santiago

A Romanesque church with the Puerta del Perdón, where sick medieval pilgrims who could go no farther could receive the same pardon as at Santiago.

Calle Santiago, 24500 Villafranca del Bierzo, León

Hotel · Villafranca del Bierzo

69. Albergue Ave Fénix

A long-running private hostel beside the church of Santiago, built and rebuilt by the Jato family. Rustic, with a communal dinner and a lot of Camino history.

Calle Santiago 10, 24500 Villafranca del Bierzo, León

Stage 26 · Villafranca del Bierzo to O Cebreiro · 28 km

Attraction · O Cebreiro

70. Santuario de Santa María a Real do Cebreiro

One of the oldest churches on the Camino, pre-Romanesque in origin, with the chalice of a medieval Eucharistic miracle. The parish priest here painted the first yellow arrows.

O Cebreiro, 27671 Pedrafita do Cebreiro, Lugo

Hotel · O Cebreiro

71. Albergue de Peregrinos do Cebreiro

The Xunta's public hostel at the top of the village, with views over the Galician hills on a clear evening. Popular, so arrive early in season.

Lugar Cebreiro, 17, 27671 Pedrafita do Cebreiro, Lugo

Museum · O Cebreiro

72. Conxunto Etnográfico do Cebreiro

Round stone houses with thatched roofs, a style of mountain dwelling older than the Romans. One palloza is kept as a small museum of village life.

O Cebreiro, 27671 Pedrafita do Cebreiro, Lugo

Stage 27 · O Cebreiro to Triacastela · 21 km

Attraction · Alto de San Roque

73. Monumento ao Peregrino

A large bronze pilgrim leaning into the wind, hat clamped down, at a pass about 4 km past O Cebreiro. The best photo stop of the morning.

LU-633, Alto de San Roque, 27671 Pedrafita do Cebreiro, Lugo

Hotel · Triacastela

74. Albergue de Peregrinos de Triacastela

The Xunta hostel in a meadow at the entrance to the village after the long descent. Triacastela's cafés and its pilgrim menus are a short walk away.

Rúa do Peregrino, 27630 Triacastela, Lugo

Stage 28 · Triacastela to Sarria via Samos · 25 km

Attraction · Samos

75. Mosteiro de Samos

One of the oldest monasteries in Spain, still home to Benedictine monks, at the bottom of a green river valley. Take the Samos route out of Triacastela for this; the monks lead guided visits.

Rúa Compostela, 27620 Samos, Lugo

Hotel · Sarria

76. Albergue de Peregrinos de Sarria

The Xunta hostel on the old main street up the hill. Sarria is the start for many walkers aiming for the last 100 km, so the trail gets busier from here.

Rúa Maior 79, 27600 Sarria, Lugo

Stage 29 · Sarria to Portomarín · 22 km

Attraction · Portomarín

77. Igrexa de San Nicolás de Portomarín

A fortified Romanesque church moved stone by stone up the hill in the 1960s when the old town was flooded for a reservoir. The numbers on the stones are still visible.

Plaza Conde Fenosa, 27170 Portomarín, Lugo

Hotel · Portomarín

78. Albergue de Peregrinos de Portomarín

The public hostel in the upper town near the church. Cross the long bridge over the Miño reservoir and climb the stairs to reach it.

Rúa Fraga Iribarne, 27170 Portomarín, Lugo

Stage 30 · Portomarín to Palas de Rei · 25 km

Museum · Castromaior

79. Castro de Castromaior

An Iron Age hill fort beside the trail with the stone footings of round houses inside its rings of walls. Free to wander, and almost no one stops.

LU-633, Castromaior, 27188 Portomarín, Lugo

Hotel · Palas de Rei

80. Albergue de Peregrinos de Palas de Rei

The Xunta hostel on the main road in the middle of town, close to cafés and shops.

Avenida de Compostela 19, 27200 Palas de Rei, Lugo

Stage 31 · Palas de Rei to Arzúa · 29 km

Restaurant · Melide

81. Pulpería Ezequiel

Melide is the octopus town of the Camino, and this long hall of shared tables is the classic stop for pulpo á feira on wooden plates with bread and young white wine.

Avenida de Lugo, 15800 Melide, A Coruña

Hotel · Arzúa

82. Albergue Municipal de Arzúa

The Xunta hostel in an old stone building on the main square of Arzúa. Pick up some of the local soft cheese for tomorrow's lunch.

Rúa Cima do Lugar 6, 15810 Arzúa, A Coruña

Stage 32 · Arzúa to O Pedrouzo · 19 km

Hotel · O Pedrouzo

83. Albergue de Peregrinos de Arca do Pino

The Xunta hostel on the main road through O Pedrouzo, the last night for most pilgrims before Santiago.

Avenida de Lugo 30, 15821 O Pedrouzo, A Coruña

Attraction · O Pedrouzo

84. Igrexa de Santa Eulalia de Arca

The parish church of Arca, just off the main road through O Pedrouzo. Many pilgrims go to the evening Mass here on their last night before Santiago.

A Igrexa, Arca, 15821 O Pino, A Coruña

Stage 33 · O Pedrouzo to Santiago de Compostela · 20 km

Park · Santiago de Compostela

85. Monte do Gozo

The hill where pilgrims got their first view of the cathedral spires, marked by a monument for the 1989 papal visit and statues of two pilgrims pointing west. About 5 km to go.

Monte do Gozo, 15892 Santiago de Compostela, A Coruña

Attraction · Santiago de Compostela

86. Catedral de Santiago de Compostela

The end of the road: the Portico of Glory, the tomb of Saint James and the hug of the apostle's statue behind the altar. Check the official site for the pilgrim Mass and when the botafumeiro swings.

Praza do Obradoiro, 15704 Santiago de Compostela, A Coruña

Attraction · Santiago de Compostela

87. Oficina del Peregrino

Where pilgrims who have walked at least the last 100 km with a stamped credential receive the Compostela. Register online before you arrive to skip much of the line.

Rúa das Carretas 33, 15705 Santiago de Compostela, A Coruña

Hotel · Santiago de Compostela

88. Parador Hostal dos Reis Católicos

A royal pilgrim hospital from 1499 on the cathedral square, now a state hotel with four cloisters. A traditional splurge for the last night.

Rúa Obradoiro, 1, 15707 Santiago de Compostela, A Coruña, España

Hotel · Santiago de Compostela

89. Albergue Seminario Menor

A huge former seminary turned hostel on a hill above the old town, with dorms and simple private rooms. A good base for a couple of days in the city.

Avenida de Quiroga Palacios 2, 15703 Santiago de Compostela, A Coruña

Attraction · Santiago de Compostela

90. Mercado de Abastos

Santiago's stone food market, with fish, shellfish, cheese and produce from Galician farms. Some stalls will cook what you buy at a counter nearby.

Rúa das Ameas s/n, 15704 Santiago de Compostela, A Coruña
