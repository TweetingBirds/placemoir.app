A week in Orlando

Seven days of theme parks and the space coast, springs and manatees, and the quieter, tree-lined side of Orlando and Winter Park.

37 places · Picked by Nate Bird · Checked November 8, 2026

Stay

Hotel · Walt Disney World

Disney's Grand Floridian Resort & Spa

Disney's grandest hotel, one monorail stop from the Magic Kingdom. Watch the fireworks from the beach.

4401 Floridian Way, Lake Buena Vista, FL 32830

Hotel · Walt Disney World

Disney's Pop Century Resort

The budget Disney pick, with the Skyliner gondola to EPCOT and Hollywood Studios.

1050 Century Dr, Lake Buena Vista, FL 32830

Hotel · Universal

Loews Royal Pacific Resort

A South Seas-style hotel with a short boat ride to Universal. A stay includes Universal Express passes.

6300 Hollywood Way, Orlando, FL 32819

Hotel · Winter Park

The Alfond Inn

A calm hotel full of contemporary art, a walk from Park Avenue. A good base away from the parks.

300 E New England Ave, Winter Park, FL 32789

Restaurant · Walt Disney World

Victoria & Albert's

Disney's tasting-menu splurge, in the Grand Floridian. Dress up and book months ahead.

4401 Floridian Way, Lake Buena Vista, FL 32830

Restaurant · Walt Disney World

Sanaa

African-Indian cooking with giraffes and zebras outside the window. Order the bread service.

3701 Osceola Pkwy, Bay Lake, FL 32830

Restaurant · Disney Springs

Raglan Road Irish Pub

Irish food, live music and dancers every night at Disney Springs.

1640 E Buena Vista Dr, Lake Buena Vista, FL 32830

Restaurant · Disney Springs

The Boathouse

Steaks and seafood on the water, with vintage amphicar rides out front.

1620 E Buena Vista Dr, Lake Buena Vista, FL 32830

Restaurant · Winter Park

The Ravenous Pig

Winter Park's gastropub, with house-made charcuterie and its own beer.

565 W Fairbanks Ave, Winter Park, FL 32789

Restaurant · Winter Park

Prato

Wood-fired pizza and pasta on Park Avenue. Sit on the sidewalk.

124 N Park Ave, Winter Park, FL 32789

Restaurant · Mills 50

Hawkers Asian Street Food

Small plates from across Asia. The roti canai and the curry laksa are favorites.

1103 N Mills Ave, Orlando, FL 32803

Restaurant · Mills 50

Pho 88

Huge bowls of pho in Orlando's Vietnamese district, cheap and quick.

730 N Mills Ave, Orlando, FL 32803

Café · Milk District

Se7en Bites

A Southern bake shop with giant breakfasts and slices of pie.

617 N Primrose Dr, Orlando, FL 32803

Restaurant · Mills 50

Black Bean Deli

Cuban sandwiches and plantains in a converted gas station.

1835 E Colonial Dr, Orlando, FL 32803

Restaurant · Downtown

Kres Chophouse

A dark, handsome downtown steakhouse in a 1930s dime store.

17 W Church St, Orlando, FL 32801

Restaurant · Audubon Park

East End Market

A small market hall with good coffee at Lineage and ramen at Domu.

3201 Corrine Dr, Orlando, FL 32803

Café · Thornton Park

Dandelion Communitea Café

A vegetarian café in an old house, with a porch and a long tea list.

618 N Thornton Ave, Orlando, FL 32803

Bar · Mills 50

Wally's Mills Ave Liquors

A 1950s dive bar with vintage pin-up wallpaper and cheap, strong drinks.

1001 N Mills Ave, Orlando, FL 32803

Attraction · Walt Disney World

Magic Kingdom Park

Rope drop for the big rides, a break at midday, then back for the fireworks.

1180 Seven Seas Dr, Lake Buena Vista, FL 32830

Attraction · Walt Disney World

EPCOT

Eat your way around the World Showcase lake, one country at a time.

200 Epcot Center Dr, Orlando, FL 32821

Attraction · Walt Disney World

Disney's Animal Kingdom Theme Park

Do the safari early, while the animals are awake, and Pandora at night.

2901 Osceola Pkwy, Bay Lake, FL 32830

Attraction · Universal

Universal Studios Florida

Ride the Hogwarts Express over to Islands of Adventure with a park-to-park ticket.

6000 Universal Blvd, Orlando, FL 32819

Attraction · Universal

Universal Islands of Adventure

Hogsmeade, Hagrid's coaster and the VelociCoaster. Go early for Hagrid's.

6000 Universal Blvd, Orlando, FL 32819

Attraction · Universal

Universal Epic Universe

Universal's newest park, with worlds for Nintendo, dragons and Harry Potter's Ministry of Magic.

1001 Epic Blvd, Orlando, FL 32819

Attraction · Space Coast

Kennedy Space Center Visitor Complex

An hour east. Stand under a Saturn V and walk past Space Shuttle Atlantis. Check the launch schedule.

Space Commerce Way, Merritt Island, FL 32953

Museum · Winter Park

Charles Hosmer Morse Museum of American Art

The world's largest Tiffany collection, including a whole chapel. Free on Friday evenings in winter.

445 N Park Ave, Winter Park, FL 32789

Museum · Loch Haven Park

Orlando Museum of Art

A compact art museum in Loch Haven Park, next to the science center.

2416 N Mills Ave, Orlando, FL 32803

Museum · Loch Haven Park

Orlando Science Center

Four floors of hands-on science and a giant dome theater. Good with kids on a park-off day.

777 E Princeton St, Orlando, FL 32803

Attraction · Winter Park

Scenic Boat Tour

An hour on pontoon boats through Winter Park's lakes and narrow canals, past lakeside mansions.

312 E Morse Blvd, Winter Park, FL 32789

Attraction · South Orlando

Gatorland

Old Florida at its most fun: thousands of alligators, a zip line and a gator-wrestling show.

14501 S Orange Blossom Trl, Orlando, FL 32837

Attraction · Disney Springs

Disney Springs

Disney's free shopping and dining district. Good on an evening off from the parks.

1486 Buena Vista Dr, Lake Buena Vista, FL 32830

Outside

Park · Downtown

Lake Eola Park

A loop around the lake and its fountain, with swans and swan boats. Sunday has a farmers market.

512 E Washington St, Orlando, FL 32801

Park · Audubon Park

Harry P. Leu Gardens

Fifty acres of camellias, roses and huge oaks draped in Spanish moss.

1920 N Forest Ave, Orlando, FL 32803

Park · Winter Park

Kraft Azalea Garden

Cypress trees and a lakeside exedra. Quiet, and lovely at sunset.

1365 Alabama Dr, Winter Park, FL 32789

Park · Apopka

Wekiwa Springs State Park

A cold, clear spring for swimming, and a river to paddle. Arrive early, as it fills up.

1800 Wekiwa Cir, Apopka, FL 32712

Park · Orange City

Blue Spring State Park

Hundreds of manatees shelter in the spring run from November to March.

2100 W French Ave, Orange City, FL 32763

Park · Lake Wales

Bok Tower Gardens

A singing carillon tower in hilltop gardens an hour south. Time your visit for a recital.

1151 Tower Blvd, Lake Wales, FL 33853
