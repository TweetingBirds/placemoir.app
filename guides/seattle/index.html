A week in Seattle

Seven days of Pike Place, ferries and salmon, glass art and rock history, and green parks with views of the Sound and the mountains.

43 places · Picked by Nate Bird · Checked November 8, 2026

Stay

Hotel · Downtown

Fairmont Olympic Hotel

Seattle's grand 1924 hotel. Classic rooms and an easy walk to the market and the art museum.

411 University St, Seattle, WA 98101

Hotel · Pike Place Market

Inn at the Market

A small hotel right at the market. Book a room facing Elliott Bay.

86 Pine St, Seattle, WA 98101

Hotel · Waterfront

The Edgewater

Built on a pier over Elliott Bay, where the Beatles once fished from their window.

2411 Alaskan Way, Seattle, WA 98121

Hotel · Belltown

Ace Hotel Seattle

Simple, stylish rooms in Belltown, some with a shared bath. The budget pick.

2423 1st Ave, Seattle, WA 98121

Restaurant · Pike Place Market

Pike Place Chowder

Award-winning chowders in a tiny spot off Post Alley. Get the New England in a bread bowl.

1530 Post Alley, Seattle, WA 98101

Restaurant · Pike Place Market

Piroshky Piroshky

Russian hand pies baked all day. The smoked salmon pâté one is the classic.

1908 Pike Pl, Seattle, WA 98101

Restaurant · Pike Place Market

Beecher's Handmade Cheese

Watch cheese being made through the window, then get the mac and cheese.

1600 Pike Pl, Seattle, WA 98101

Restaurant · Pike Place Market

Matt's in the Market

Seasonal Northwest dinners upstairs in the market, looking out at the famous clock.

94 Pike St, Seattle, WA 98101

Restaurant · Pike Place Market

The Pink Door

Italian-American food behind an unmarked pink door, with a deck over the bay.

1919 Post Alley, Seattle, WA 98101

Restaurant · Waterfront

Elliott's Oyster House

Dozens of Northwest oysters on Pier 56. Come for the afternoon oyster happy hour.

1201 Alaskan Way, Seattle, WA 98101

Restaurant · Capitol Hill

Taylor Shellfish Oyster Bar

A shellfish farm's own oyster bar. Geoduck if you're brave, Kumamotos if you're not.

1521 Melrose Ave, Seattle, WA 98122

Restaurant · Pioneer Square

Salumi

Cured meat sandwiches in Pioneer Square. The porchetta is worth the lunch line.

404 Occidental Ave S, Seattle, WA 98104

Restaurant · Queen Anne

Canlis

The special night out since 1950, with a glass dining room above Lake Union. Book well ahead.

2576 Aurora Ave N, Seattle, WA 98109

Restaurant · Fremont

Paseo

Caribbean roast pork sandwiches dripping with caramelized onions. Grab extra napkins.

4225 Fremont Ave N, Seattle, WA 98103

Restaurant · Ballard

Un Bien

The Paseo family's own sandwich shack in Ballard. Pair it with a walk at the Locks.

7302 15th Ave NW, Seattle, WA 98117

Restaurant · Wallingford

Dick's Drive-In

Cheap burgers, hand-cut fries and shakes since 1954. A Seattle rite of passage.

111 NE 45th St, Seattle, WA 98105

Café · Wallingford

Molly Moon's Homemade Ice Cream

Local ice cream with a line even in the rain. Salted caramel is the bestseller.

1622 N 45th St, Seattle, WA 98103

Café · Capitol Hill

Starbucks Reserve Roastery

A theatrical roastery with copper casks and coffee you can't get anywhere else.

1124 Pike St, Seattle, WA 98101

Café · Capitol Hill

Caffe Vita

The Capitol Hill roastery behind a lot of Seattle's coffee. Strong espresso, no fuss.

1005 E Pike St, Seattle, WA 98122

Bar · Pike Place Market

Zig Zag Café

A cocktail bar tucked down the market stairs. It revived the Last Word, so order one.

1501 Western Ave, Seattle, WA 98101

Bar · Fremont

Fremont Brewing

A family-friendly beer garden near the canal. Try the Interurban IPA.

1050 N 34th St, Seattle, WA 98103

Attraction · Pike Place Market

Pike Place Market

Fish throwers, flower stalls and the gum wall. Come early on a weekday before the crowds.

85 Pike St, Seattle, WA 98101

Attraction · Seattle Center

Space Needle

The 1962 World's Fair tower, with a rotating glass floor. Go on a clear day for Mount Rainier.

400 Broad St, Seattle, WA 98109

Museum · Seattle Center

Chihuly Garden and Glass

Dale Chihuly's glass art in galleries, a glasshouse and a garden. Stunning at dusk.

305 Harrison St, Seattle, WA 98109

Museum · Seattle Center

Museum of Pop Culture

Jimi Hendrix, Nirvana and sci-fi, in Frank Gehry's crumpled metal building.

325 5th Ave N, Seattle, WA 98109

Museum · Downtown

Seattle Art Museum

Strong Northwest Coast Native art, and the giant Hammering Man out front.

1300 1st Ave, Seattle, WA 98101

Attraction · Downtown

Seattle Central Library

Rem Koolhaas's glass-and-steel library. Ride to the tenth floor and spiral down.

1000 4th Ave, Seattle, WA 98104

Attraction · Waterfront

Seattle Aquarium

Sea otters, octopus and the creatures of Puget Sound, on Pier 59.

1483 Alaskan Way, Seattle, WA 98101

Museum · Pioneer Square

Klondike Gold Rush National Historical Park

A free museum on how Seattle outfitted the 1897 gold rush. Good with a Pioneer Square walk.

319 2nd Ave S, Seattle, WA 98104

Museum · Chinatown-International District

Wing Luke Museum

Asian Pacific American history in an old hotel. Take the tour of the restored rooms.

719 S King St, Seattle, WA 98104

Attraction · Waterfront

Seattle Ferry Terminal

Ride the ferry to Bainbridge Island for the cheapest skyline cruise in town.

801 Alaskan Way, Seattle, WA 98104

Museum · South Lake Union

Museum of History & Industry

Seattle's story from logging to Boeing to grunge, in an old naval armory on Lake Union.

860 Terry Ave N, Seattle, WA 98109

Attraction · Ballard

Hiram M. Chittenden Locks

Watch boats rise and fall, and salmon climb the fish ladder from July to September.

3015 NW 54th St, Seattle, WA 98107

Attraction · Fremont

Fremont Troll

A giant troll crushing a real Volkswagen under the Aurora Bridge. Climb on for a photo.

N 36th St, Seattle, WA 98103

Museum · University District

Burke Museum

Natural history and Native culture, with working labs you can watch through the glass.

4300 15th Ave NE, Seattle, WA 98105

Museum · Boeing Field

The Museum of Flight

Walk through a Concorde and Air Force One. Worth half a day.

9404 E Marginal Way S, Seattle, WA 98108

Outside

Park · Magnolia

Discovery Park

The city's biggest park. Walk the loop trail down to the beach and the West Point lighthouse.

3801 Discovery Park Blvd, Seattle, WA 98199

Park · Queen Anne

Kerry Park

The postcard view of the Space Needle and Mount Rainier. Best at sunset.

211 W Highland Dr, Seattle, WA 98119

Park · Wallingford

Gas Works Park

An old gas plant turned park on Lake Union. Fly a kite and watch the seaplanes land.

2101 N Northlake Way, Seattle, WA 98103

Park · Belltown

Olympic Sculpture Park

Free outdoor sculpture zigzagging down to the water, with the Olympics across the Sound.

2901 Western Ave, Seattle, WA 98121

Park · Capitol Hill

Volunteer Park

A Victorian conservatory, a water tower to climb and the Asian Art Museum.

1247 15th Ave E, Seattle, WA 98112

Park · Montlake

Washington Park Arboretum

Miles of trails through trees from around the world, and a lovely Japanese garden.

2300 Arboretum Dr E, Seattle, WA 98112

Park · West Seattle

Alki Beach Park

A long sandy beach with skyline views. Take the water taxi over from downtown.

1702 Alki Ave SW, Seattle, WA 98116
